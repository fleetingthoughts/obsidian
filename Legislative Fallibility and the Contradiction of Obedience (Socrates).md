When Thrasymachus defines justice as the advantage of the stronger who set the laws, how does Socrates use the _requirement of obedience_ to force a logical contradiction?
#### Notes
Thrasymachus concedes that rulers are fallible and sometimes legislate laws disadvantageous to themselves (339c). Because the ruled must obey all laws, they are legally compelled to execute actions that are _disadvantageous_ to the stronger, contradicting the premise (339d–e).
