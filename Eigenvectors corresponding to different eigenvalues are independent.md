Let $\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_k$ be distinct eigenvalues of $T$. If $S_i$ is a linearly independent set of eigenvectors for $\lambda_i$, what is the status of the linear dependence $\bigcup_{i=1}^k S_i$?
#### Notes
The union $\bigcup_{i=1}^k S_i$ is linearly independent.
