What is the formal logical structure of a Deductive-Nomological (D-N) explanation, and how is it represented?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** A D-N explanation is a deductive argument where the explanandum sentence $E$ is logically deduced from a set of explanans premises. The explanans consists of two classes of statements: general laws of universal form $L_1, L_2, \dots, L_r$ and statements of particular facts $C_1, C_2, \dots, C_k$. The formal schema is: $$\begin{aligned} L_1, L_2, \dots, L_r & \quad \text{(General Laws)} \\ C_1, C_2, \dots, C_k & \quad \text{(Particular Facts)} \\ \hline E & \quad \text{(Explanandum Sentence)} \end{aligned}$$ (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 5, p. 51)
