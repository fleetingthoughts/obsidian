State the two conditions required to verify a linear operator $T$ on an $n$-dimensional space is diagonalizable using the rank of $(T - \lambda I)$.
#### Notes
- The characteristic polynomial of $T$ splits.
- For each eigenvalue $\lambda$, its algebraic multiplicity equals $n - \text{rank}(T - \lambda I)$.
