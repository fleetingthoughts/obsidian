What is the formal stylistic distinction between simple narrative and imitation (_mimesis_)?
#### Notes
n simple narrative, the poet speaks in their own voice without hiding themselves, keeping a third-person perspective; in imitation (_mimesis_), the poet speaks in the direct voice of a character, likening their own voice and style to that of another (Book 3, 392c–393c).
