Let $g: [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$ be bounded. What property characterizes $g$ if there exists a single partition $P$ of $[a, b]$ such that $L(g, P) = U(g, P)$?
#### Notes
$g$ is constant on $[a, b]$.
