State the condition for the limit of a sequence of functions to be continuous.
#### Notes
Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence of functions defined on $A \subseteq \mathbf{R}$ that converges uniformly on $A$ to $f$. If each $f_n$ is continuous at $c \in A$, then $f$ is continuous at $c$.
