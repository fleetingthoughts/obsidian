Let $T$ be an invertible linear operator. Prove rigorously that a scalar $\lambda$ is an eigenvalue of $T$ if and only if $\lambda^{-1}$ is an eigenvalue of $T^{-1}$.
#### Notes
Because $T$ is invertible, an eigenvalue $\lambda$ cannot be $0$. $T(v) = \lambda v \iff v = T^{-1}(\lambda v) = \lambda T^{-1}(v) \iff \lambda^{-1}v = T^{-1}(v)$.
