What is the causal relationship between a healthy soul and a healthy body?
#### Notes
**Premise.** Socrates asserts the starting psychological premise that a healthy body does not make the soul good by its own physical virtue, but rather a good soul, through its own virtue, makes the physical body as good as it can possibly be (Book 3, 403c–d)
