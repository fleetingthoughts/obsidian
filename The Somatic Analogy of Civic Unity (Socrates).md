Elaborate on how the abolition of private property and family structures actively achieves the city's greatest good, utilizing the analogy of the human body and the specific benefits.
#### Notes
By removing private families and wealth, citizens uniformly apply the terms "my own" and "not my own" to the same things (462c, 464c–d). This unifies the city so that it functions like a single human body; when one part (a citizen) suffers or prospers, the entire community feels the pain or pleasure as a whole (462d–e).
