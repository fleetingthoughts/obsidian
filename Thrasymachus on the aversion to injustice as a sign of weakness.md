Why do those who condemn injustice do so, according to Thrasymachus's psychological diagnosis?
#### Notes
Thrasymachus asserts that people who reproach injustice do so not because they have a moral aversion to doing unjust deeds, but because they are afraid of suffering those deeds themselves (Book 1, 344c).
