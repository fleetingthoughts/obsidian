What is the defining administrative and psychological character of the Timocratic state?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Timocracy is a structural middle between aristocracy and oligarchy, imitating both while possessing unique traits of its own.<br>• _Argument_: While it imitates aristocracy in honoring rulers and maintaining common meals and gymnastics, it is argued to possess unique defects: it is afraid to bring wise men to ruling offices because its intellectuals are no longer simple and earnest, leaning instead toward simpler, spirited men naturally suited for war. It honors military stratagems, spends its time making war, and fosters a secret, fierce desire for gold and silver, which the rulers hoard in private treasuries (Book 8, 547d–548c).
