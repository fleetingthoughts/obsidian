State the existence and uniqueness theorem for the adjoint of a linear operator $T$ on a finite-dimensional inner product space $V$.
#### Notes
There exists a unique function $T^*: V \to V$ such that $\langle T(x), y \rangle = \langle x, T^*(y) \rangle$ for all $x, y \in V$; furthermore, $T^*$ is a linear operator.
