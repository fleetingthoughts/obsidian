What hypothesis on $f : [a, b] \to \mathbf{R}$ is required alongside integrability on $[c, b]$ for all $c \in (a, b)$ to ensure $f$ is Riemann-integrable on $[a, b]$?
#### Notes
$f$ must be bounded on the closed interval $[a, b]$.
