State Pauppus-Guldin's rule regarding the surface area and volume of a solid of revolution
#### Notes
**Significance:** Simplifies the calculation of surface areas and volumes of complex solids of revolution without requiring explicit integration, provided the centroid and basic geometric properties of the generating shape are known.
- **First Theorem (Surface Area):** The surface area $A$ generated by rotating a plane curve about a non-intersecting axis is equal to the product of the curve's arc length $L$ and the distance traveled by its geometric centroid ($2\pi \bar{y}$). Formula: $A = 2\pi \bar{y} L$.
- **Second Theorem (Volume):** The volume $V$ generated by rotating a plane area about a non-intersecting axis is equal to the product of the area $A$ and the distance traveled by its geometric centroid ($2\pi \bar{y}$). Formula: $V = 2\pi \bar{y} A$.
