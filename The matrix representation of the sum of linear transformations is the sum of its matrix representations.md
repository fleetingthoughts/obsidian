State how the matrix representation of the sum of linear transformations can be determined from the matrix representation of the individual transformations
#### Notes
For transformations $T, U$ and scalar $a$: (a) $[T+U]_\beta^\gamma = [T]_\beta^\gamma + [U]_\beta^\gamma$ (b) $[aT]_\beta^\gamma = a[T]_\beta^\gamma$
