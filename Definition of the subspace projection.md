Given subspace $W_1$ and $W_2$ in $V$. Define the subspace projection of a vector $v \in V$ on $W_1$ along $W_2$.
#### Notes
Let $W_1$ and $W_2$ be subspaces of $V$ such that $V = W_1 \oplus W_2$. The function $T: V \rightarrow V$ defined by $T(x) = x_1$, where $x = x_1 + x_2$ with $x_1 \in W_1$ and $x_2 \in W_2$, is defined as the projection of $V$ on $W_1$ along $W_2$.
