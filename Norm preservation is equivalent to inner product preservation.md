Prove: Let $T$ be a linear operator on an inner product space $V$. Then $\Vert{}T(x)\Vert{} = \Vert{}x\Vert{}$ for all $x \in V$ if and only if $\langle T(x), T(y) \rangle = \langle x, y \rangle$ for all $x, y \in V$.
