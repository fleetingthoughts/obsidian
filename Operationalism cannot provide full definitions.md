Why can operational criteria provide only "partial interpretations" rather than full definitions for scientific terms?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Epistemological Premise.** An operational criterion (such as using a mercury thermometer to determine temperature) is applicable only within a limited range of physical circumstances (e.g., within the liquid range of mercury). Consequently, the operational procedure does not fully define or exhaust the meaning of the scientific term across all possible conditions, providing instead only a partial interpretation of the concept. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 7, p. 98)
