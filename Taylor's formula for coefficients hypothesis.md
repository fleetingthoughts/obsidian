What hypothesis on $f$ is required to guarantee that its power series coefficients at zero are uniquely determined by $a_n = \frac{f^{(n)}(0)}{n!}$?
#### Notes
$f(x)$ must possess a convergent power series expansion on a nontrivial open interval $(-R, R)$ centered at zero (mere infinite differentiability at $0$ is insufficient).
