Define the determinant and the characteristic polynomial of a linear operator
#### Notes
Let $T$ be a linear operator on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$. Choose any ordered basis $\beta$ for $V$. The determinant of $T$, denoted $\det(T)$, is defined to be the determinant of $A = [T]_\beta$, and the characteristic polynomial $f(t)$ of $T$ is defined to be the characteristic polynomial of $A$; that is, $f(t) = \det(A - tI_n)$.
