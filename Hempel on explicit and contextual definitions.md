What is the difference between an explicit definition and a contextual definition (interpretation) in Hempel's analysis of theoretical terms?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Distinction.** An explicit definition fully specifies the meaning of a term in isolation (e.g., 'acid' means 'electrolyte that furnishes hydrogen ions'). A contextual definition specifies the meaning of a term only within a particular sentential context or phrase (e.g., we cannot operationally define the term 'hardness' or 'electron' by itself, but we can contextually interpret phrases like 'mineral $m_1$ is harder than mineral $m_2$' or 'this condensation track in the cloud chamber marks the path of an electron'). (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 7, pp. 98–99)
