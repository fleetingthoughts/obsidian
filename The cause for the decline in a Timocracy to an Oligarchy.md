How does the accumulation of private gold in the Timocratic state destroy the regime and pave the way for Oligarchy?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Virtue and wealth are in direct tension, functioning like weights on a scale where as one rises, the other must fall.<br>• _Argument_: Because timocratic citizens accumulate gold in private treasure houses and seek lavish expenditures, they pervert the laws to suit their desires. As they compete in wealth accumulation, they make the multitude like themselves, eventually honoring money-making and the wealthy above virtue. It is argued that this shift in values destroys the timocratic honor-system, leading the city to formally establish wealth as the sole criterion for political power (Book 8, 550d–551a).
