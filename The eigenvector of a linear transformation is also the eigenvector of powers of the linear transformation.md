If $x$ is an eigenvector of a linear operator $T$ with eigenvalue $\lambda$, what is the eigenvalue of $x$ under $T^m$ for a positive integer $m$?
#### Notes
The proof is done by induction and the corresponding eigenvalue of $x$ for $T^m$ is  $\lambda^m$
