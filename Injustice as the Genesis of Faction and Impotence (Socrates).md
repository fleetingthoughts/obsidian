When Thrasymachus argues that tyranny and large-scale injustice generate ultimate power, what _inherent internal mechanism_ of injustice does Socrates identify to prove it causes impotence?
#### Notes
Socrates proves injustice inherently implants hatred, quarrels, and factions (351d). Whether in a city, a gang, or an individual soul, it divides the entity against itself, making it an enemy to itself and incapable of unified action (351e–352a).
