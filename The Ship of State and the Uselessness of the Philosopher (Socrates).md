The specific conclusion drawn from the "Ship of State" analogy regarding the philosopher's perceived uselessness in the city.
#### Notes
- **Premise:** The true pilot (the philosopher) is rendered useless solely because the ignorant sailors (politicians) drug the shipowner (the populace) to seize control, refusing to employ the pilot's art (488a-488e).
    
- **Conclusion:** The philosopher is not to blame for this uselessness, as it is unnatural for the wise pilot to beg to rule over the ignorant (489b).
