What is "empirical import" (cognitive significance), and how does a pseudo-hypothesis fail to meet this standard?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** Empirical import is a statement's capacity to have a bearing on, or explain, empirical phenomena by yielding test implications. A pseudo-hypothesis fails this because it has no test implications whatsoever; for example, explaining gravity as "natural appetites akin to love or hatred" makes no specific empirical assertions, meaning no observational data can confirm or disconfirm it, leaving it devoid of explanatory power. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 3, pp. 30-31)
