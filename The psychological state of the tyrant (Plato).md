What 3 things define the psychology of a tyrant
#### Notes
**Premise.** Socrates establishes the clinical premise that a human being becomes tyrannic in the precise, absolute sense when, either by nature, habit, or both, their soul exists in a state of being simultaneously **drunken, erotic, and melancholic** (Book 9, 573c).
