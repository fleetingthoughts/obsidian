What is the tripartite identity of the "good life" that Socrates and Crito agree remains unchanged, and why is it foundational?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition**: Definition / Foundational Axiom. **Philosophical Concept**: Eudaimonism / Ethical Intellectualism. **Inferential Logic**: Socrates defines the "good life," the "beautiful life," and the "just life" as identical. This serves as a vital premise: mere survival (life) is not the highest good; only a life lived in accordance with justice and virtue has intrinsic value. Consequently, any choice to survive (by escaping) must first pass the test of justice. **Citation**: _Crito_, 48b [Passage 5].
