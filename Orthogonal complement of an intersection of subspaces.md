Prove: If $W_1$ and $W_2$ are subspaces of a finite-dimensional inner product space $V$, then $(W_1 \cap W_2)^\perp = W_1^\perp + W_2^\perp$.
#### Notes
Apply the orthogonal complement of a sum to $W_1^\perp$ and $W_2^\perp$: $(W_1^\perp + W_2^\perp)^\perp = (W_1^\perp)^\perp \cap (W_2^\perp)^\perp = W_1 \cap W_2$. Taking orthogonal complements of both sides yields $W_1^\perp + W_2^\perp = (W_1 \cap W_2)^\perp$.
