Define the $N$th-degree Taylor polynomial $S_N(x)$ of an infinitely differentiable function $f$ centered at $a \in \mathbf{R}$.
#### Notes
$$S_N(x) = \sum_{n=0}^N \frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!} (x-a)^n$$
