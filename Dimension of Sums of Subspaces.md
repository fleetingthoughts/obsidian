If $W_1$ and $W_2$ are finite-dimensional subspaces of a vector space $V$, then the sum $W_1 + W_2$ is finite-dimensional and satisfies the dimension formula $\dim(W_1 + W_2) = \dim(W_1) + \dim(W_2) - \dim(W_1 \cap W_2)$.
