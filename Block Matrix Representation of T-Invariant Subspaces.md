Let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional vector space and $T: V \to V$ be a linear transformation. If $V$ contains a $T$-invariant subspace $W$ of dimension $k$, what special matrix representation form can be found for $T$, and what are the properties of its component blocks?
#### Notes
Let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional vector space, and let $T: V \to V$ be a linear transformation. If $W$ is a $T$-invariant subspace of $V$ having dimension $k$, there is a basis $\beta$ such that $[T]_\beta$ has the block upper triangular form $\begin{pmatrix} A & B \\ O & C \end{pmatrix}$, where $A$ is a $k \times k$ matrix and $O$ is the $(n-k) \times k$ zero matrix.
