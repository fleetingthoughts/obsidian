What is the philosophical definition and logical structure of a "crucial test" (experimentum crucis) designed to adjudicate between two rival theories?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** A crucial test is an experiment designed to decide between two rival hypotheses H1 and H2 that have stood up equally well. It requires finding a test condition C under which the theories predict mutually exclusive outcomes: "If C then E1" and "If C then E2", where E1 and E2 cannot both occur. Performance of the test theoretically refutes one hypothesis and supports the other. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 3, pp. 25-26)
