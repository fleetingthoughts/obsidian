Assuming $A$ and $B$ are subspaces (or subsets containing the zero vector), state the subset relationship between their union $A \cup B$ and their sum $A + B$
#### Notes
Let $A$ and $B$ be subsets of a vector space $V$ that both contain the zero vector, $0$. The relationship is that the union is a subset of the sum:
<!--SR:!fsrs,2026-10-03T22:53:10.725Z,8,8.2956,1,2,1,0,0,2026-09-25T22:53:10.725Z-->

$$A \cup B \subseteq A + B$$
