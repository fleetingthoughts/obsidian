State the equation of the Tangent Line at a point $(x,y)$ of a parametrically defined curve where derivatives do not vanish simultaneously.
#### Notes
$(\xi-x)\dot{y} - (\eta-y)\dot{x} = 0$, where $(\xi,\eta)$ are running coordinates.
