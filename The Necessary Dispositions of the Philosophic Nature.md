The causal mechanism explaining why a true lover of learning necessarily becomes moderate and completely devoid of the love of money.
#### Notes
- **Premise:** When human desires are channeled intensely toward one specific thing, such as the pleasure of the soul, they are proportionally drained away from the pleasures of the body (485d).
    
- **Conclusion:** Therefore, a true philosopher's desires are starved of physical luxury and wealth, making them naturally moderate by default (485e).
