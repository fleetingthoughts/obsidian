State the equivalent sequence-based definition for the limit point of a set.
#### Notes
$x$ is a limit point of $A$ if and only if $x = \lim a_n$ for some sequence $(a_n)$ contained in $A$ satisfying $a_n \neq x$ for all $n \in \mathbf{N}$.
