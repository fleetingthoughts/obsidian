State the Mean value theorem for a function $f : [a, b] \to \mathbf{R}$.
#### Notes
If $f$ is continuous on $[a, b]$ and differentiable on $(a, b)$, then there exists a point $c \in (a, b)$ where $f'(c) = \frac{f(b) - f(a)}{b - a}$.
