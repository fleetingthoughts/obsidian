As the debate escalates, how does Thrasymachus categorically redefine perfect injustice in relation to virtue and wisdom?
#### Notes
He abandons customary usage and claims that perfect injustice is "good counsel" (wisdom) and a virtue, while justice is merely high-minded innocence (348c–e).
