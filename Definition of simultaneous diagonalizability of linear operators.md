Define what it means for two linear operators $T$ and $U$ on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$ to be simultaneously diagonalizable.
#### Notes
There exists an ordered basis $\beta$ for $V$ such that both $[T]_\beta$ and $[U]_\beta$ are diagonal matrices.
