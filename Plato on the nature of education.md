How does Socrates' definition of education (_paideia_) contrast with the sophists' model of instruction?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: The power to learn and the organ of understanding are already naturally present in every human soul (Book 7, 518c).<br>• _Argument_: Because knowledge cannot be inserted into a soul that lacks it (just as sight cannot be put into blind eyes), it is argued that true education is not the instillation of knowledge, but rather the art of turning (_periagoge_) the whole soul around from the changing world of Becoming until it is strong enough to look directly at absolute Being and the Good (Book 7, 518b–d).
