What is the logical syllogism of a scientific hypothesis's test implications according to Hempel?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** Test implications are conditional statements of the form "If conditions of kind C are realized, then an event of kind E will occur". They express material implications (conditionals) derived from a hypothesis, asserting that under specified test conditions, a particular outcome of a certain kind will occur. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, pp. 19-20)
