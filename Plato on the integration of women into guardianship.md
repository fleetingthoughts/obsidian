Why is the introduction of female guardians argued to be the greatest benefit to the city?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: There is no greater good for a city than the coming to be within it of the best possible men and women (Book 5, 456e).<br>• _Argument_: Because a shared education in music and gymnastics is the only method that produces the highest-quality citizens, and because this education acts on the same human nature in both sexes, it is argued that educating female guardians is possible and represents the highest good for the city's preservation (Book 5, 456c–457a).
