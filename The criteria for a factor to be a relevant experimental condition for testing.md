What determines whether a particular way of varying experimental conditions is regarded as scientifically important or pointless?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Methodological Rule.** The classification of certain experimental variations as important or pointless is based on the background assumptions (or theories) we currently entertain concerning which factors are likely to affect the phenomenon in question. For example, Pascal's brother-in-law repeated the mountain barometer experiment under wind, cover, and rain because the relevant meteorological factors were still unknown. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 4, p. 36)
