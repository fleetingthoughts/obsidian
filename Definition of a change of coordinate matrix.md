Define a change of coordinate matrix.
#### Notes
Let $\beta$ and $\beta'$ be ordered bases for a finite-dimensional vector space $V$. The change of coordinate matrix that changes $\beta'$-coordinates into $\beta$-coordinates is defined as $Q = [I_V]_{\beta'}^\beta$
