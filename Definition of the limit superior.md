Define the limit superior of a sequence $(a_n)$.
#### Notes
$\limsup_{n \to \infty} a_n = \lim_{n \to \infty} (\sup_{k \ge n} a_k)$. Equivalently, it is the supremum of the set of all subsequential limits of the sequence.
