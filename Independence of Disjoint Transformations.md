Let $V$ and $W$ be vector spaces, and let $T$ and $U$ be nonzero linear transformations from $V$ into $W$. If the intersection of their ranges is strictly trivial, meaning $R(T) \cap R(U) = \{0\}$, it forces a specific structural relationship between the transformations themselves.
#### Notes
The set containing the transformations, $\{T, U\}$, forms a linearly independent subset within the vector space $\mathcal{L}(V, W)$.
