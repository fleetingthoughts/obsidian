How does Socrates logically dismantle the eristic claim that male and female biological differences necessitate different civic duties?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: We must distinguish between relevant and irrelevant forms of sameness and difference when applying the Principle of Specialization (Book 5, 454b–d).<br>• _Argument_: Socrates uses the analogy of bald versus long-haired shoemakers to show that physical differences are irrelevant to a person's craft capacity. Because the biological difference between men and women applies only to reproduction (the female bears and the male mounts), and does not affect the soul's capacity for an art, it is argued that this difference does not justify assigning them different civic duties (Book 5, 454c–455a).
