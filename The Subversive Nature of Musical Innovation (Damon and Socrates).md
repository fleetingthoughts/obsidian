Identify the specific, systemic danger Socrates associates with innovation in the arts, specifically music and gymnastic and why we must pay special attention to its protection
#### Notes
Relying on Damon, Socrates asserts that the modes of music are never moved without moving the greatest political laws (424c). Lawless play eventually flows into dispositions, subverts private and public contracts, and finally destroys the regime itself (424d–e)
