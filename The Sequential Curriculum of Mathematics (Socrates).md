The five-part procedural sequence of preparatory mathematical studies required to draw the soul toward truth before attempting dialectic.
#### Notes
- **Calculation/Number:** Distinguishing the one, two, and three; forces the soul to contemplate pure, indivisible numbers (525a-d).
    
- **Plane Geometry:** The study of two-dimensional surfaces, pursued for the sake of knowing eternal being, not practical action (526c-e).
    
- **Solid Geometry:** The study of the third dimension (cubes/depth), which is currently neglected due to city ignorance but is logically necessary (528b-d).
    
- **Astronomy:** The study of solids in motion; must be pursued using intellectual problems rather than merely gaping up at the physical ceiling of the sky (528e-530c).
    
- **Harmonics:** The antistrophe to astronomy; the study of numerical concordances, achieved through intellectual problems rather than torturing physical strings by ear (530d-531c)
