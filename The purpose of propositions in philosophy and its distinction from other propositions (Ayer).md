What is the precise epistemological status of philosophical propositions in Ayer's framework, and how do they differ from both scientific hypotheses and lexicographical entries?
#### Notes
**Nature of Propositions**: Epistemological Partition & Definition of Philosophy.<br><br>**Definition**: Philosophical propositions are analytic statements that express definitions of symbols or exhibit the formal logical consequences of linguistic conventions. Philosophy is a department of logic.<br>**Distinction vs. Natural Science**:<br>1. _Science_: Formulates synthetic empirical hypotheses describing physical or mental objects, validated by sensory observation.<br>2. _Philosophy_: Formulates analytic logical statements regarding symbolic relations; scientific and philosophical statements cannot contradict one another because they belong to different logical types.<br>**Distinction vs. Lexicography**:<br>1. _Lexicography_: Generates empirical synthetic propositions regarding how specific words are actually used by a historical group of speakers.<br>2. _Philosophy_: Formulates analytic propositions governing the logical equivalences and rules for whole classes of expressions.<br>**Citation**: Chapter 2, Pages 57, 70.
