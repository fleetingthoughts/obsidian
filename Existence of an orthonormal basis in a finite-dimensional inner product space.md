Prove: Every nonzero finite-dimensional inner product space $V$ possesses an orthonormal basis.
#### Notes
Choose a basis for $V$, apply the Gram–Schmidt process to obtain an orthogonal basis of nonzero vectors, and divide each vector by its norm.
