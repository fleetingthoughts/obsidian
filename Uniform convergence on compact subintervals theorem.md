State the relationship between absolute convergence of a power series at a point and uniform convergence.
#### Notes
If a power series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n x^n$ converges absolutely at a point $x_0$, then it converges uniformly on the closed interval $[-c, c]$, where $c = \vert{}x_0\vert{}$.
