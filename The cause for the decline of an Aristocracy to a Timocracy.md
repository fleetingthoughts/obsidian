How does the failure of mathematical calculation and the "geometric number" lead to the decline of the Aristocracy?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Everything that has come into being is subject to decay, and human births are governed by a perfect "geometric number" that controls the quality of begettings.<br>• _Argument_: Because the rulers cannot perfectly grasp this geometric number through sensory perception and calculation, they will inevitably couple brides and grooms out of season. This produces children lacking good natures, who subsequently neglect music and gymnastics. Rulers chosen from this class will fail to preserve the purity of Hesiod's metallic races, leading to a chaotic mixing of iron with silver and bronze with gold, which naturally engenders unlikeness, irregularity, war, and civic faction (Book 8, 546a–547a).
