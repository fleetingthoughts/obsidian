Define uniform convergence of an infinite series of functions $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f_n(x)$ to a function $f(x)$ on a set $A$.
#### Notes
The series converges uniformly on $A$ to $f(x)$ if the sequence of partial sums $s_k(x) = \sum_{n=1}^k f_n(x)$ converges uniformly to $f(x)$ on $A$.
