Let $\mathcal{P}$ be the collection of all partitions of $[a, b]$ and let $f: [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$ be bounded. Define the upper integral $U(f)$ and lower integral $L(f)$.
#### Notes
$U(f) = \inf\{U(f, P) : P \in \mathcal{P}\}$ and $L(f) = \sup\{L(f, P) : P \in \mathcal{P}\}$
