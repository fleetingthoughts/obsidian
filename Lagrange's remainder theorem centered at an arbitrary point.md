State Lagrange's remainder formula for $E_N(x)$ when the Taylor polynomial $S_N(x)$ is centered at $a \in \mathbf{R}$.
#### Notes
If $f$ is differentiable $N+1$ times on an interval containing $a$ and $x$ ($x \neq a$), there exists $c$ strictly between $a$ and $x$ such that:

$$E_N(x) = \frac{f^{(N+1)}(c)}{(N+1)!} (x-a)^{N+1}$$
