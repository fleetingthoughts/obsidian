Given that $S_1 \subseteq S_2 \subseteq V$, state the relationship between $span(S_1)$ and $span(S_2)$ and prove it. If $span(S_1)=V$, what can we say about $span(S_2)$?
#### Notes
Any $v \in \text{span}(S_1)$ is a finite linear combination of vectors in $S_1$. Because $S_1 \subseteq S_2$, $v$ is also a linear combination of vectors in $S_2$, forcing $v \in \text{span}(S_2)$.
