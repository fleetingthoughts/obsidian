Prove: If $f$ is differentiable on $[a, b]$ with a continuous derivative $f'$, then $f$ is Lipschitz on $[a, b]$.
#### Notes
The continuous function $\vert{}f'\vert{}$ attains a maximum $M$ on $[a, b]$. By the Mean value theorem, $\left\vert{} \frac{f(x) - f(y)}{x - y} \right\vert{} = \vert{}f'(c)\vert{} \le M$, satisfying the Lipschitz condition.
