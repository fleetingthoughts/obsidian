State how the matrix representation of the composition of linear representations can be determined from the matrix representation of the individual linear transformations
#### Notes
Let $T:V \to W$ and $U:W \to Z$ be linear with ordered bases $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ for $V, W, Z$ respectively. Then $[UT]_\alpha^\gamma = [U]_\beta^\gamma [T]_\alpha^\beta$.
