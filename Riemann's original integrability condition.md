State Riemann's original tagged-partition definition for $\int_a^b f = A$.
#### Notes
For every $\epsilon \gt 0$, there exists $\delta \gt 0$ such that for any tagged partition $(P, \{c_k\})$ of $[a, b]$ satisfying $\Delta x_k \lt \delta$ for all $k$, $\vert{}R(f, P) - A\vert{} \lt \epsilon$.
