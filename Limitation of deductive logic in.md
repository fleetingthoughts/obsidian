Why does pure deductive logic operating solely on immediate sense-experience fail to yield knowledge of unobserved reality?
#### Notes
**Premise**: Valid deductive conclusions must be logically contained within their premises.<br>**Humean Principle**: No single observed empirical event logically entails or points to any distinct unobserved past, present, or future event.<br>**Conclusion**: Inferences to unobserved facts require synthetic general rules established inductively. Operating solely on immediate data, pure deduction cannot advance a single step beyond present experience. _(Chapter 2, pp. 47–48)_
