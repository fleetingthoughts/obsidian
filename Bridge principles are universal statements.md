Why must the view that individual theoretical terms (like 'electron') possess a finite number of individual operational criteria be abandoned, and what replaces it?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Logical Argument.** Individual theoretical terms cannot be assigned a finite list of operational criteria because the statements containing them yield an unlimited diversity of test implications. Instead of interpreting theoretical terms individually, a theory relies on a set of **bridge principles** that connect the unobservable theoretical entities and processes to macroscopic, pretheoretical observables, providing an indefinite variety of criteria of application. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 7, pp. 99–100)
