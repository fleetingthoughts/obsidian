Why is the second stage of narrow inductivism (classifying facts without any pre-existing hypotheses) logically blind according to Hempel?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Methodological Argument.** A set of facts can be classified in an infinite number of unilluminating ways (e.g., classifying maternity patients by hair color, age, or marital status). To lead to a valid explanation, a particular way of analyzing and classifying findings must be guided by a hypothesis about how the phenomena are connected. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 2, pp. 12–13)
