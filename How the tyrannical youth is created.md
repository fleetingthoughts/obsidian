How is the tyrannical youth created from their environment
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: A democratic father maintains a moderate compromise between oligarchic stinginess and lawless, luxurious desires (Book 9, 572c–d).<br>• _Argument_: When the son is exposed to corrupting enchanters who seek to lead him into complete lawlessness (disguised as "complete freedom"), and who find that other relatives are bolstering his father's moderate middle path, they contrive to implant a dominant, insatiable desire—characterized as a **"great winged drone"**—to lead his idle, resource-consuming desires. This drone, fed by surrounding luxuries, plants the sting of longing, takes madness as its bodyguard, and purges the youth's soul of all moderation and shame (Book 9, 572d–573b).
