State the strong differentiable limit theorem for a sequence of differentiable functions $(f_n)$ on $[a, b]$.
#### Notes
If $(f_n')$ converges uniformly to $g$ on $[a, b]$ and there exists a point $x_0 \in [a, b]$ where $(f_n(x_0))$ converges, then $(f_n)$ converges uniformly on $[a, b]$ to a differentiable function $f$ satisfying $f' = g$.
