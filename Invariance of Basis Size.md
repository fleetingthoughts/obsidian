Prove that the basis of a vector space has a unique number of vectors.
#### Notes
Let $V$ be a vector space having a finite basis. Then all bases for $V$ are finite, and every possible basis for $V$ contains the exact same number of vectors. _(Note: This is the specific corollary that legally allows us to define the "dimension" of a space as a fixed integer)_
