Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional inner product space and $T$ be a linear operator on $V$. Define the adjoint $T^*: V \to V$.
#### Notes
The unique function $T^*: V \to V$ satisfying:

$$\langle T(x), y \rangle = \langle x, T^*(y) \rangle \quad \text{for all } x, y \in V$$
