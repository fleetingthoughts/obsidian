Provide the definition of separated sets.
#### Notes
Two nonempty sets $A, B \subseteq \mathbf{R}$ are separated if $A \cap \bar{B}$ and $\bar{A} \cap B$ are both empty, where $\bar{A}$ and $\bar{B}$ denote the closures of $A$ and $B$, respectively.
