What is Hempel's logical argument proving that scientific hypotheses cannot be mechanically generated from empirical data using "rules of induction"?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Epistemological Argument.** Scientific theories are usually couched in **theoretical terms** (e.g., "atom", "electron", "sea of air") that do not appear at all in the description of the empirical findings they explain (e.g., gas spectra or barometer readings). No general mechanical rule of induction can be expected to systematically invent novel, non-empirical concepts to explain pre-existing macroscopic data. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 2, p. 13
