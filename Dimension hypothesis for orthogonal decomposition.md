Let $W$ be a subspace of an inner product space $V$. What dimension hypothesis on $W$ and $V$ suffices to guarantee that every $y \in V$ decomposes uniquely as $y = u + z$ with $u \in W$ and $z \in W^\perp$?
#### Notes
$W$ must be finite-dimensional ($V$ itself need not be finite-dimensional).
