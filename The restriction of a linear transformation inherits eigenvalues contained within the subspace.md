Prove: If $W$ is a $T$-invariant subspace and $v \in W$ is an eigenvector of $T_W$ with eigenvalue $\lambda$, then $v$ is an eigenvector of $T$ with eigenvalue $\lambda$.
#### Notes
Since $v \in W$, $T(v) = T_W(v)$. Given $T_W(v) = \lambda v$, it follows that $T(v) = \lambda v$.
