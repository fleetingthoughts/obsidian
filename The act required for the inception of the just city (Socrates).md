The radical act that is the fastest and easiest method for the true philosophers to bring the ideal city and its regime into actual existence.
#### Notes
- **Premise:** Current adult citizens possess deeply ingrained, corrupt dispositions inherited from their own upbringing (540e-541a).
    
- **Pivot:** To wipe the tablet of the city clean, the philosophers must banish everyone older than ten years of age to the countryside (540e).
    
- **Conclusion:** By taking over only the pure children, the philosophers can rear them entirely within the new manners and laws, bypassing the corruption of the parents and establishing the regime rapidly (541a).
