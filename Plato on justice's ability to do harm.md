How does Socrates use the concept of specific virtue (_arete_) to refute the claim that it is just to harm bad men or enemies?
#### Notes
Harming any entity (like a horse or a dog) necessarily degrades it with respect to its specific virtue (Book 1, 335b–c). Since justice is the specific virtue of human beings, harming a human being necessarily makes them more unjust (Book 1, 335c). Because an opposite cannot produce its opposite (e.g., heat cannot cool, dryness cannot wet), a just person cannot use his virtue—justice—to make another person more unjust (Book 1, 335c–d).
