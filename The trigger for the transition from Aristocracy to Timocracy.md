What is the compromising agreement that formally transitions the state from Aristocracy to Timocracy?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: The rise of metallic faction divides the citizens, with the iron and bronze races seeking private wealth, while the gold and silver races lead toward virtue and the ancient establishment.<br>• _Argument_: Because the two factions are in tension, they come to a middle agreement: they distribute land and houses to be held privately, enslave the citizens they previously guarded as free friends, holding them as serfs and domestics, and dedicate themselves entirely to war and guarding against internal rebellion (Book 8, 547b–c).
