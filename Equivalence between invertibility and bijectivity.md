State the equivalent condition between the bijectivity and invertibility of a linear transformation
#### Notes
For a linear transformation $T: V \to W$ between finite-dimensional vector spaces of equal dimension, $T$ is invertible $\iff$ $T$ is one-to-one $\iff$ $T$ is onto.
