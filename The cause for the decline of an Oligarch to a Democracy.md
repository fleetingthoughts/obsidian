How does the insatiable desire for wealth in Oligarchy logically trigger the transition to Democracy?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: It is impossible for a city to honor wealth and adequately maintain moderation among its citizens simultaneously.<br>• _Argument_: Because the oligarchs want to become as rich as possible, they refuse to pass laws restraining licentious youth from wasting their property, choosing instead to buy their estates and lend them money on interest. This compels honorable young men to become poor, armed debtors with stings who sit idly in the city plotting revolution. When these poor observe the soft, fat, and physically weak rich panting in battle, they realize the rich are weak. It is argued that on the slightest pretext, the poor win by killing or exiling their opponents, establishing democracy (Book 8, 555b–557a).
