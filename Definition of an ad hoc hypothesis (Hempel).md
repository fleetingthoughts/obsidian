What is the definition of an "ad hoc hypothesis" in scientific methodology, and what is its primary epistemic function?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** An ad hoc hypothesis is an auxiliary assumption introduced for the sole purpose of saving a core hypothesis from threatening adverse evidence. It is not called for by other empirical findings and does not lead to any new, independent test implications beyond the specific phenomenon it was introduced to explain. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 3, pp. 28-29)
