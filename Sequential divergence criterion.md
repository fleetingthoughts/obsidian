State the criterion used to prove that a functional limit $\lim_{x\to c} f(x)$ does not exist using different sequences in the domain
#### Notes
If there exist two sequences $(x_n)$ and $(y_n)$ in $A$ with $x_n \neq c$ and $y_n \neq c$ such that $\lim x_n = \lim y_n = c$ but $\lim f(x_n) \neq \lim f(y_n)$, then the functional limit $\lim_{x\to c} f(x)$ does not exist.
