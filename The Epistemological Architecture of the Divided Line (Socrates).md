Map the ontological divisions of the "Divided Line," distinguishing the specific objects found within the visible and intelligible realms.
#### Notes
The line is cut into two unequal segments: the visible and the intelligible (509d). The visible contains images (shadows and reflections) and the physical things they imitate (animals, plants, artifacts) (509e–510a). The intelligible contains mathematical objects/hypotheses (where the soul uses physical images to reach a conclusion) and the Forms themselves (where the soul uses no physical images, moving from a hypothesis to an unhypothetical beginning) (510b–511c
