Let $T$ be a linear operator on a finite-dimensional inner product space $V$ and let $\beta$ be an orthonormal basis for $V$. How is $[T^*]_\beta$ related to $[T]_\beta$?
#### Notes
$$[T^*]_\beta = ([T]_\beta)^*$$
