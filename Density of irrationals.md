Prove that the irrationals are infinitely dense assuming the density of rationals
#### Notes
We make use of a lemma: the sum of a irrational number and rational number must result in an irrational number.

The goal is to show that there exists an irrational number $a < i<b$ for any real numbers $a<b$. By the density of rationals, there exists a rational number $r$ such that $a-\sqrt{2} < r< b-\sqrt{2}$. Then  shifting the equality by adding $\sqrt{2}$  and with our lemma, we note that $r+\sqrt{2}$ is an irrational number that sits between any reals $a<b$
