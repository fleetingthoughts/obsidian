What is "support from above" (theoretical support) in Hempel's confirmation framework, and how is it logically structured?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** Theoretical support occurs when a hypothesis receives credibility not from direct inductive-evidential data, but from more inclusive, well-confirmed theories that deductively imply the hypothesis in conjunction with specific background facts. For example, a law of free fall on the moon is theoretically supported because it is deductively derived from Newton's theory of gravitation and motion, combined with the moon's mass and radius. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 4, p. 38)
