State the formula for the radius of convergence $R$ using the coefficients of a power series $\sum a_n x^n$
#### Notes
If $L = \lim_{n \to \infty} \vert{}a_{n+1} / a_n\vert{}$ exists and $L > 0$, then $R = 1/L$. If $L = 0$, $R = \infty$. This also holds using the limit superior.
