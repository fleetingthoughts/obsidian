State the convergence geometry theorem for a power series evaluated at a point $x_0$
#### Notes
If a power series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n x^n$ converges at some point $x_0 \in \mathbf{R}$, then it converges absolutely for any $x$ satisfying $\vert{}x\vert{} < \vert{}x_0\vert{}$.
