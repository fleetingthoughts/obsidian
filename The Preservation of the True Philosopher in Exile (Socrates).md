Detail the specific conditions under which a true philosopher survives in a corrupt city, and how they navigate their civic existence.
#### Notes

A small band of true philosophers remains due to physical illness keeping them out of politics (like Theages' bridle), divine signs (like Socrates' sign), or being a great soul in a small city (496b–c). Recognizing the madness of the many, the survivor keeps quiet and minds his own business, like a man taking shelter under a wall in a dust storm, content to live pure of injustice and depart with fair hope (496d–e).
