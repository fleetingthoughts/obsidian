How does the insatiable desire for freedom in a Democracy logically prepare the transition to Tyranny?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Excess in anything usually provokes a violent, correspondingly great change in the opposite direction.<br>• _Argument_: When a democracy thirsts for freedom and gets bad winebearers as leaders, it gets drunk on unmixed liberty and punishes anyone who is moderate as a "willing slave" or "oligarch". Anarchy filters down into private houses and even beasts: fathers fear sons, sons have no shame, teachers fawn on pupils, and animals bump into people on the road. It is argued that this extreme, tender sensitivity to any form of restraint causes the citizens to pay no attention to written or unwritten laws to avoid having any master; thus, the extreme of freedom naturally changes into the greatest and most savage slavery—Tyranny (Book 8, 562b–564a).
