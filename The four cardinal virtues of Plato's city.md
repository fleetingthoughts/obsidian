What are the 4 cardinal virtues of a city and how does Socrates argue where they reside with respect to how they characterize a well-governed city? How does he argue for justice differently?
#### Notes
To distinguish which class the virtue belongs to, we examine if we still have a well-governed city if the virtue resides in any of the other classes
- Wisdom resides with the rulers because only their knowledge matters. Wisdom in a carpenter makes for good carpentry but does not make a city better governed (428b–429a).
- Courage resides with the soldiers and he defines it as a form of preservation(429b-430c). 
- Moderation resides with all 3 classes and is form of subservience to the rulers (430d-432a).
- By process of elimination, the last remaining virtue required for a well-governed city must be justice (432b-434c)
