Prove: In an inner product space over $F$, $\langle x, cy \rangle = \overline{c}\langle x, y \rangle$.
#### Notes
$\langle x, cy \rangle = \overline{\langle cy, x \rangle} = \overline{c \langle y, x \rangle} = \overline{c} \overline{\langle y, x \rangle} = \overline{c} \langle x, y \rangle$.
