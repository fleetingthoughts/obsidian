State the term-by-term differentiability theorem for a series of functions.
#### Notes
Let $f_n$ be differentiable functions defined on an interval $A$. If $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f_n'(x)$ converges uniformly to $g(x)$ on $A$, and there exists a point $x_0 \in A$ where $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f_n(x_0)$ converges, then $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f_n(x)$ converges uniformly to a differentiable function $f(x)$ satisfying $f'(x) = g(x)$ on $A$.
