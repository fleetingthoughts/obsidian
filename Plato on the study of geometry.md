Why is the study of geometry (_geometria_) essential for the guardians, and what is its true epistemic purpose?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Geometry must be pursued for the sake of knowing what is eternal, rather than what comes into being and passes away (Book 7, 527a–b).<br>• _Argument_: While practitioners ridiculously speak as though they are performing physical actions (squaring, adding, applying), it is argued that because geometrical knowledge is of "what is always," its greater and more advanced part naturally draws the soul upward, making it easier to contemplate the Idea of the Good (Book 7, 526d–527c).
