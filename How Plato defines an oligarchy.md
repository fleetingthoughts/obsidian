What is the formal defining mechanism of the Oligarchic regime?
#### Notes
**Premise.** Socrates establishes the starting definition that an Oligarchy is a regime founded strictly on a property assessment, where the rich rule and the poor man has no share in the ruling offices, a law they put into effect either by force of arms or by arousing fear (Book 8, 550c, 551a–b).
