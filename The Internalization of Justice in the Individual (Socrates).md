Define the state of justice within the individual soul, mirroring the established macrocosm of the city.
#### Notes
Individual justice is internal harmony where each of the three parts minds its own business: the calculating part rules with wisdom, the spirited part allies with it via courage, and all three agree harmoniously (moderation) to master the desiring part (441d–443e).
