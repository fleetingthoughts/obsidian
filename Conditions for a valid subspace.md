What are the three conditions of the Subspace Criterion for a subset $W \subseteq V$?
#### Notes
1. $0 \in W$
2. $x + y \in W$ whenever $x, y \in W$. 
3. $cx \in W$ whenever $c \in F$ and $x \in W$.
