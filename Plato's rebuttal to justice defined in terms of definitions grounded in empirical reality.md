What logical contradiction is exposed when Polemarchus agrees that "friends are those who seem good, and enemies are those who seem bad"?
#### Notes
Because human beings make mistakes in judgment, they often believe bad people are good and good people are bad (Book 1, 334c). Under this definition, it would be just for these mistaken individuals to harm their good friends (whom they mistake for bad enemies) and help their bad enemies, which directly contradicts the premise that justice should help friends and harm enemies (Book 1, 334c–e).
