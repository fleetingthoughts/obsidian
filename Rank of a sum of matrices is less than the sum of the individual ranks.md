Prove for any $m \times n$ matrices $A$ and $B$, $\text{rank}(A+B) \le \text{rank}(A) + \text{rank}(B)$.
#### Notes
Observe $R(L_{A+B}) = R(L_A + L_B) \subseteq R(L_A) + R(L_B)$. Taking the dimension of both sides yields the inequality.
