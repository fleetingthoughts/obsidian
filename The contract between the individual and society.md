What is Socrates' ethical rule regarding agreements, and how does it apply to his relationship with the laws of Athens?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition**: Premise / Moral Axiom. **Philosophical Concept**: _Pacta Sunt Servanda_ (agreements must be kept) / Moral Contractualism. **Inferential Logic**: Socrates asserts that when one has made a "just agreement" (an agreement not entered into under compulsion, deceit, or pressure of time), one must fulfill it and not cheat on it. The unstated assumption is that Socrates' long-standing residence in Athens constitutes a voluntary, just agreement to abide by its judicial and legislative verdicts. **Citation**: _Crito_, 49e–50a [Passage 7].
