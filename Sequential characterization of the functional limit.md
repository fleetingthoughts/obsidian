State the equivalence theorem connecting the functional limit $\lim_{x\to c} f(x) = L$ to sequential limits, including all necessary boundary conditions on the sequences.
#### Notes
The functional limit $\lim_{x\to c} f(x) = L$ if and only if for all sequences $(x_n) \subseteq A$ satisfying $x_n \neq c$ for all $n \in \mathbf{N}$ and $(x_n) \to c$, it follows that the image sequence satisfies $f(x_n) \to L$.
