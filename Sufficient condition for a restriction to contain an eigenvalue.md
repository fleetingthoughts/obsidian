Prove: If the characteristic polynomial of $T$ splits, any nontrivial $T$-invariant subspace $W$ contains an eigenvector of $T$.
#### Notes
The characteristic polynomial of $T_W$ divides that of $T$, so it also splits. Thus, $T_W$ has at least one eigenvalue and a corresponding nonzero eigenvector in $W$. By eigenvalue inheritance, this is an eigenvector of $T$.
