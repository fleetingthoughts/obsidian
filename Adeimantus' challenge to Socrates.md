What is the specific challenge Adeimantus lays down to Socrates, distinguishing it from all previous defenses of justice?
#### Notes
Socrates must show what justice and injustice each do by their own power in the soul of the possessor—whether noticed by gods and humans or not—and prove why justice is the greatest good and injustice the greatest evil, entirely ignoring reputations, wages, and consequences (Book 2, 366d–367e).
