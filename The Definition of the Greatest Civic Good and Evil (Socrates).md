Identify the ultimate teleological standard by which the legislator must judge the organization of a city.
#### Notes
The greatest evil is what splits a city and makes it many instead of one (faction) (462a–b). The greatest good is what binds it together and makes it one, specifically the community of pleasure and pain where all citizens rejoice or grieve together at the same events (462b).
