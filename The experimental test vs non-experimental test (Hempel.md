How does Hempel methodologically distinguish between an experimental test and a non-experimental test of a hypothesis?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Methodological Distinction.** An experimental test is possible when the test conditions C are technologically realizable and can be actively brought about at will, allowing control of variables. A non-experimental test must be employed when experimental control is impossible, requiring the scientist to seek out or wait for nature to realize the conditions C. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 3, pp. 19-21)
