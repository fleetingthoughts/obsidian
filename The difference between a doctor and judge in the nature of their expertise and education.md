How must a doctor acquire medical expertise, and why does personal illness not hinder their capability and how does that contrast with a judge's education and expertise
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Doctors do not cure the physical body with the body, but cure the body with the soul (Book 3, 408d).<br>• _Argument_: Because the soul is the active healing agent, and because a soul cannot be corrupted by physical illness, it is argued that a doctor's personal physical sickness actually aids their medical training by providing a practical, first-hand understanding of physical suffering (Book 3, 408d–e).

**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: A judge rules a soul with a soul, and a corrupt, vicious soul is incapable of making healthy judgments about virtue or justice (Book 3, 408d–409a).<br>• _Argument_: Because a judge’s soul is the direct instrument of justice, it is argued that it must remain completely untainted and innocent of bad character in youth, learning about injustice only late in life as an alien, external phenomenon observed in others (Book 3, 409a–c)
