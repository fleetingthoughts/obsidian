What is the relationship between the logical (or inductive) probability of a hypothesis and Rudolf Carnap's concept of "degree of confirmation"?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** Logical or inductive probability expresses the degree of rational credibility or evidential support that a body of statements K lends to a hypothesis H. Carnap formalized this relation by defining a quantitative "degree of confirmation" c(H, K) in model languages of simple logical structure, showing that this logical relation satisfies all the basic mathematical axioms of probability theory. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 4, pp. 44-46)
