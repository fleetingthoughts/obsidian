What is the core methodological thesis of P. W. Bridgman's **operationism** regarding scientific concepts?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Epistemological Premise.** Operationism asserts that the meaning of every scientific term must be specifiable by indicating a definite testing operation that provides an objective, unequivocal criterion for its application. Under this view, a concept is completely synonymous with its corresponding set of testing operations (e.g., the concept of length is synonymous with the set of operations by which length is measured). (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 7, pp. 88–91)
