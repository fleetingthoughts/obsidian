What is the personified Laws' argument regarding the existential threat posed by Socrates' potential escape?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition**: Reductio ad Absurdum / Dialectical Counter-Argument. **Philosophical Concept**: The Rule of Law / Institutional Existentialism. **Inferential Logic**: The Laws argue that: (1) if private individuals can nullify and set aside the verdicts of the courts, then the verdicts of the courts have no force; (2) a city cannot exist or avoid destruction if its court verdicts have no force; (3) by escaping, Socrates is attempting to nullify a court verdict; (4) therefore, Socrates' escape is an attempt to destroy the laws and the city itself. **Citation**: _Crito_, 50a–c [Passage 7].
