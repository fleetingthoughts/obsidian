How does Polemarchus interpret the poet Simonides’ definition of justice as "giving to each what is owed"?
#### Notes
Polemarchus claims that friends owe it to friends to do some good and nothing bad, while enemies owe to enemies what is fitting, which is some harm (Book 1, 332a–d).
