What is Hans Reichenbach's inductive approximation argument for the justification of simplicity, and what are Hempel's two objections to it?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Epistemological Argument.** Reichenbach argues that if there is a true functional connection between variables, continuously drawing the simplest (smoothest) curve through progressively more measured data points will gradually converge toward the true curve. Hempel objects that (1) this procedure provides no indication of how close to the true curve we are at any given stage, and (2) other non-simple procedures (like connecting points with semicircles) would also mathematically guarantee convergence, yet are not scientifically sound. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 4, pp. 42-43
