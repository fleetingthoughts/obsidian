What are the four stages of the "ideal" scientific inquiry under the narrow inductivist (Baconian) conception of science?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** The four stages are: (1) observation and recording of all facts without selection or prior guesses, (2) analysis and classification of these facts without hypotheses, (3) inductive derivation of generalizations regarding their connections, and (4) further testing of these generalizations. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 2, p. 10)
