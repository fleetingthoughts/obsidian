Outline Adeimantus's initial objection regarding the lifestyle of the guardians and their lack of conventional happiness.
#### Notes
Adeimantus objects that the guardians, despite being the true masters of the city, are deprived of wealth, private property, and the ability to travel or entertain, living merely as unpaid mercenary watchmen (419a–420a).
