How does the 1956 discovery of parity violation by Tsung-Dao Lee and Chen-Ning Yang demonstrate the revolutionary value of questioning "pointless" background assumptions?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Historical Case Example.** The principle of parity assumed that the laws of nature are impartial between right and left (i.e., that a physical process and its mirror image are equally possible). By questioning this background assumption—which was previously considered too solid to vary—Lee and Yang proposed that parity is violated in weak interactions, which was soon experimentally confirmed and overthrew a fundamental physical paradigm. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 4, p. 36)
