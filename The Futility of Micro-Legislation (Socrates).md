Describe Socrates's medical analogy refuting the need for a good city to legislate minor social conventions and market behaviors.
#### Notes
Badly governed cities endlessly legislate minor contracts and behaviors, acting like sick men who constantly try new drugs without abandoning their licentious lifestyle (425e–426a). In a properly educated city, citizens will naturally dictate these conventions without written laws, as like calls forth like (425a–c).
