State the $\epsilon$-partition criterion for the Riemann integrability of a bounded function $f: [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$. This is also referred to as the Darboux integrability criteria
#### Notes
$f$ is integrable on $[a, b]$ if and only if for every $\epsilon \gt 0$, there exists a partition $P_\epsilon$ of $[a, b]$ such that $U(f, P_\epsilon) - L(f, P_\epsilon) \lt \epsilon$
