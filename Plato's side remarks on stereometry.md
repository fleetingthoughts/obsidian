Why does Socrates initially skip stereometry (solid geometry) in his curriculum, and how does he justify its placement?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: The correct scientific order of study must move from the second dimension (plane surfaces) to the third dimension (depth/solids), before investigating solids in motion (astronomy) (Book 7, 528a–b).<br>• _Argument_: Because no city currently honors solid geometry, and because its study is exceptionally difficult and lacks proper supervision, Socrates initially skipped it (Book 7, 528b–c). However, because solid geometry has immense natural charm and grows perforce despite being despised, it is argued that it must be placed as the third study, preceding astronomy (Book 7, 528c–e).
