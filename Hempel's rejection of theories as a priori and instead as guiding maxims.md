If mechanism cannot be established as a true thesis a priori, how should it be construed, and what is its methodological function?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Methodological Definition.** Mechanism is best construed not as a specific scientific theory or thesis, but as a **heuristic maxim**—a guiding principle for research. This principle enjoins scientists to persist in searching for basic physico-chemical explanations of biological phenomena rather than resigning themselves to the vitalistic view that the principles of physics and chemistry are inherently powerless to account for the phenomena of life. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 8, p. 105)
