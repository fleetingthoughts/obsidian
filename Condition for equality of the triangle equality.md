State the necessary and sufficient condition for equality in the triangle inequality $\vert{}x + y\vert{} = \vert{}x\vert{} + \vert{}y\vert{}$.
#### Notes
Equality holds if and only if $x = 0$, $y = 0$, or $x = c y$ for some real scalar $c > 0$.
