How does the democratic Champion of the People transform into a Tyrant?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: A leader who tastes human blood must necessarily transform from a human being into a beast (the myth of Lycaean Zeus).<br>• _Argument_: When the leader of the people takes over a particularly obedient mob and does not hold back from shedding kindred blood, bringing false charges to murder citizens in court, and hinting at debt cancellation and land redistribution, he is fated to either be slain by his enemies or become a tyrant. It is argued that once he is exiled and returns by force, or stands victorious over his fallen rivals, he demands the notorious tyrannical request—a personal bodyguard—fully converting him into a perfected tyrant (Book 8, 565d–566d).
