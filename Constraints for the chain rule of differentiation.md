State the hypotheses required to apply the chain rule to the composition $g \circ f$ at a point $c \in A$.
#### Notes
Let $f : A \to \mathbf{R}$ and $g : B \to \mathbf{R}$. The range inclusion $f(A) \subseteq B$ must hold, $f$ must be differentiable at $c \in A$, and $g$ must be differentiable at $f(c) \in B$.
