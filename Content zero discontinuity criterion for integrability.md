State the sufficient condition for the Riemann integrability of a bounded function $f$ on $[a, b]$ in terms of content zero.
#### Notes
If $f$ is bounded on $[a, b]$ and the set of points where $f$ is discontinuous has content zero, then $f$ is Riemann-integrable on $[a, b]$.
