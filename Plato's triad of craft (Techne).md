What are the three arts associated with any craft? How does the relationship between the three arts establish the complete absence of truth in the imitator's mind? (Book 10, 601c–602b)
#### Notes
The 3 arts are using, making and imitating
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: The virtue, beauty, and correctness of any instrument or action are determined strictly by the _use_ for which it was made or grew (601d).<br>• _Argument_: Because of this, (1) the **user** must have direct **knowledge** (_episteme_) of the instrument through practical experience, which they report to the maker (601e–602a); (2) the **maker** acquires **right trust** (_orthon pistin_) by listening to and obeying the user's instructions (601e); and (3) the **imitator** has neither knowledge (since he does not use the thing) nor right opinion (since he is not forced to associate with the user to receive prescriptions) (602a–b). Therefore, it is argued that the imitator's art is completely devoid of cognitive validity (Book 10, 602a–b).
