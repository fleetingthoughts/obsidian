Prove that the curvature is invariant to rotation
#### Notes
Let the rotated coordinates be $X = x\cos\alpha + y\sin\alpha$ and $Y = -x\sin\alpha + y\cos\alpha$.

- **Denominator:** Expanding $X'^2 + Y'^2$ cancels the cross-terms, leaving $(x'^2+y'^2)(\cos^2\alpha+\sin^2\alpha) = x'^2+y'^2$.
    
- **Numerator:** Expanding $X'Y'' - Y'X''$ cancels the cross-terms, leaving $(x'y''-y'x'')(\cos^2\alpha+\sin^2\alpha) = x'y''-y'x''$.
    
- **Conclusion:** Both parts of the formula are strictly preserved.
