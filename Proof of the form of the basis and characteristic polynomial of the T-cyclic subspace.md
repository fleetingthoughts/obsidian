Prove: Let $W$ be the $T$-cyclic subspace of $V$ generated by a nonzero vector $v \in V$, with $k = \dim(W)$. Then $\{v, T(v), \dots, T^{k-1}(v)\}$ is a basis for $W$, and if $a_0 v + \dots + T^k(v) = 0$, the characteristic polynomial of $T_W$ is $f(t) = (-1)^k (a_0 + a_1 t + \dots + t^k)$.
#### Notes
- Let $j$ be the largest integer such that $\beta = \{v, T(v), \dots, T^{j-1}(v)\}$ is linearly independent.
    
- Show $\text{span}(\beta)$ is $T$-invariant, deduce $\text{span}(\beta) = W$, and conclude $\beta$ is a basis for $W$ with $j = k$.
    
- Express $T^k(v)$ as a linear combination of basis vectors to form the matrix representation $[T_W]_\beta$, which is a companion matrix.
    
- Evaluate $\det([T_W]_\beta - tI_k)$ via cofactor expansion to derive $f(t) = (-1)^k(a_0 + a_1 t + \dots + t^k)$.
