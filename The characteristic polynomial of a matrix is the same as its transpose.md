Given the characteristic polynomial $f(t)$ of a matrix $A$, what is the characteristic polynomial of $A^t$ and prove it.
#### Notes
They are identical; $A$ and $A^t$ share the exact same characteristic polynomial. You prove by using the following theorems:
- $\det(A)=\det(A^t)$
- $(A-B)^t=A^t -B^t$
