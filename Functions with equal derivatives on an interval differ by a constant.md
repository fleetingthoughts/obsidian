State the relationship between two functions with identical derivatives on an interval.
#### Notes
If $f$ and $g$ are differentiable on an interval $A$ and $f'(x) = g'(x)$ for all $x \in A$, then $f(x) = g(x) + k$ for some constant $k \in \mathbf{R}$.
