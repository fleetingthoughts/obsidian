Prove: If $A \in \mathcal{M}_{m \times n}(F)$ has $\text{rank}(A) = n$, then $A^* A$ is invertible.
#### Notes
Since $A^* A$ is an $n \times n$ matrix and $\text{rank}(A^* A) = \text{rank}(A) = n$, $A^* A$ has full rank $n$ and is therefore invertible.
