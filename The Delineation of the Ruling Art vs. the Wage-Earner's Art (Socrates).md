When Thrasymachus uses the shepherd analogy to prove rulers exploit their subjects, how does Socrates use the _separation of capacities_ to isolate the true motivation of rule?
#### Notes
Socrates separates the "shepherd's art" (which strictly cares for the sheep) from the "wage-earner's art" (which brings financial benefit) (345c–346d). Because ruling provides no inherent benefit to the ruler, good men will not rule voluntarily; they must be compelled by a penalty (the fear of being ruled by a worse man) (346e–347c).
