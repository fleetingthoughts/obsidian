How does the spatial metaphor of "up, down, and middle" explain the cognitive error of mistaking relief from pain for pleasure?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Up, down, and middle exist as objective regions in nature (Book 9, 584d).<br>• _Argument_: If a man is brought from the downward region to the middle, he will look at the place he left and believe he is in the "up" region because he has never seen what is truly above. Similarly, it is argued that those who are inexperienced in truth, when moving from pain to the painless middle state, seriously believe they are experiencing true pleasure and fulfillment because they have no experience of real, unadulterated pleasure (Book 9, 584d–585a).
