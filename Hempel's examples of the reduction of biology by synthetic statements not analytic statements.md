Why does Hempel classify the molecular characterization of organic substances like penicillin, testosterone, and cholesterol as empirical, biochemical discoveries rather than analytic definitions?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Epistemological Argument.** These characterizations are arrived at by chemical analysis rather than meaning analysis, meaning they express empirical laws of nature rather than statements of synonymy. Furthermore, redefining these biological terms by their molecular structure alters both their meaning (intension) and their application (extension), as it qualifies lab-synthesized substances as penicillin or testosterone even if they were never produced by organic systems. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 8, pp. 102–103)
