What is "support from above" (theoretical support), and how does it allow a statement to qualify as a universal law even if it has zero actual instances in the universe?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** Theoretical support occurs when a statement of universal form is logically implied by an accepted scientific theory. A statement like "On any celestial body with the same radius as Earth but twice its mass, free fall from rest conforms to $s = 32t^2$" is a genuine law even if no such celestial body exists, because it is deductively derived from Newton's laws of gravitation and motion conjoined with known gravitational constants. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 5, p. 57)
