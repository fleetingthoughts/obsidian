How does Ayer refute the dialectical objection that philosophical analysis commits one to a metaphysical ontology of "reductionist atomism"?
#### Notes
**Nature of Propositions**: Metaphilosophical Neutrality & Syntactical Definition.<br><br>**Dialectical Objection**: Critics argue that philosophical analysis relies on the metaphysical premise that the world is an aggregate of isolated "bare particulars" obtained by physically dissecting objects into simple parts.<br>**Ayer's Refutation**:<br>1. _Premise 1_: Physical dissection of objects into spatial components is an empirical scientific task, not a philosophical one.<br>2. _Premise 2_: Philosophical analysis is not a process of physical or ontological breakdown; it is the logical translation of sentences into equivalent sentences of a clearer logical form.<br>3. _Unstated Assumption_: Translating statements about material objects into statements about sense-contents makes no claim that the world is composed of isolated physical blocks.<br>**Conclusion**: Philosophical analysis is purely linguistic and syntactical.
