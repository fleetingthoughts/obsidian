Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional inner product space with an orthonormal basis $\beta = \{v_1, v_2, \dots, v_n\}$, and let $T$ be a linear operator on $V$. State the formula for the $(i, j)$-entry of $A = [T]_\beta$.
#### Notes
$$A_{ij} = \langle T(v_j), v_i \rangle.$$
