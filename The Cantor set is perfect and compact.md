What are the set properties of the Cantor set and prove them
#### Notes
The Cantor set $C = \bigcap_{n=0}^\infty C_n$ is perfect because for any $x \in C$ and $n \in \mathbf{N}$, there exists $x_n \in C \cap C_n$ with $x_n \neq x$ and $\vert{}x - x_n\vert{} \le 1/3^n$, establishing that every point of $C$ is a limit point of $C$.
