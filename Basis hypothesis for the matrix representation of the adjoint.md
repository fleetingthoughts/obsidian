What condition must a basis $\beta$ of a finite-dimensional inner product space $V$ satisfy to guarantee that $[T^*]_\beta = ([T]_\beta)^*$ for every linear operator $T$ on $V$?
#### Notes
$\beta$ must be an orthonormal basis for $V$.
