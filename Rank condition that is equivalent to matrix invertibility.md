What is the necessary and sufficient rank condition for an $n \times n$ matrix $A$ to be invertible?
#### Notes
$\text{rank}(A) = n \iff \dim(R(L_A)) = n \iff L_A: F^n \to F^n$ is surjective $\iff L_A$ is an isomorphism (since domain and codomain share the same dimension) $\iff A$ is invertible.
