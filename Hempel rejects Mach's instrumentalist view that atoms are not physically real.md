What is Ernst Mach's instrumentalist argument against the physical reality of atoms, and why does Hempel find this positivist restriction arbitrary?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Epistemological Critique.** Mach argued that scientific theories should only refer to entities and processes that are potential facts directly accessible to our senses, viewing atoms as mere mathematical models for representing experiences. Hempel argues that limiting science to observables would make it impossible to formulate precise, general explanatory laws. Since theoretical entities are tested and confirmed in the exact same inductive way as observable hypotheses, rejecting them is arbitrary. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 6, p. 81)
