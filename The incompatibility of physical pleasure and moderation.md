How does Socrates argue that excessive physical pleasure (especially sexual pleasure) is incompatible with moderation?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Moderation is defined by self-mastery and intellectual order, whereas excessive physical pleasure disrupts rational thought and puts men out of their minds just as much as acute pain does (Book 3, 402e).<br>• _Argument_: Because excessive pleasure is fundamentally a state of physical and mental frenzy, it is argued that true, moderate love must be entirely free from licentious, physical frenzy, and must instead be a musical and orderly love of what is beautiful (Book 3, 402e–403b).
