Define the foundational epistemic distinction Socrates uses to justify why philosophers, rather than non-philosophers, must be the leaders of the city.
#### Notes
Philosophers are those who can grasp what is always the same in all respects, whereas non-philosophers wander among the many and varying particulars (484b). Denying rule to philosophers is logically akin to choosing a blind man to guard a thing over someone with clear sight and a precise, true pattern in their soul (484c–d).
