How does Oligarchy generate the "disease" of the "drone" class within the city?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: In an oligarchy, citizens are legally permitted to sell all their property, leaving them without any social craft or function in the state.<br>• _Argument_: Because a person who has sold their property ceases to be a moneymaker, craftsman, knight, or hoplite, they remain in the city as out-and-out beggars. Just as a drone growing up in a hive cell is a disease of the hive, this functionless class is argued to be a disease of the city, dividing into stingless drones (beggars) and stinging drones (wrongdoers, thieves, and cutpurses) (Book 8, 552a–d).
