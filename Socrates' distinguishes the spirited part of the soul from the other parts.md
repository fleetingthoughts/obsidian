What 3 stories or real-world examples does Socrates and his interlocutors give to distinguish the spirited part from the calculating and the appetite of the soul.
#### Notes
Socrates gives the following examples (439e–441c):
- **The Story of Leontius (Spirit vs. Appetite)** Leonidus was overcome by a morbid desire to see corpses.
- ***Comparison with beasts (Spirit vs Rationality).*** Glaucon acknowledges his definition for it distinguishes beasts who are brave but do not possess the rationality.
- ***Homer's Odysseus (Spirit vs Calculation).***
