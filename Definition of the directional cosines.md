Give the direction cosines for the Angle of Inclination ($\alpha$) of a tangent line.
#### Notes

**Vector Definitions**
$\cos\alpha = \frac{\dot{x}}{\sqrt{\dot{x}^2+\dot{y}^2}}$, $\sin\alpha = \frac{\dot{y}}{\sqrt{\dot{x}^2+\dot{y}^2}}$.
