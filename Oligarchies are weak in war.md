Why is an Oligarchic state systemically weak when waging foreign war?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Waging war requires either mobilizing the populace or spending significant capital.<br>• _Argument_: Because the rulers fear the armed poor multitude more than the enemy, they cannot mobilize them without risking internal revolution. Conversely, because they love money above all else, they are unwilling to contribute their own private capital to fund the war. It is argued that this double constraint leaves the oligarchy structurally impotent on the field of battle (Book 8, 551d–e).
