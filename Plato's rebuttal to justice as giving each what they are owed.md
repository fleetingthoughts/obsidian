What is the logical induction Socrates uses to deduce that Polemarchus's definition of justice implies a just man is a kind of thief?
#### Notes
He establishes that whoever is cleverest at guarding against a blow in boxing, a disease in medicine, or an enemy's plans in war is also the one cleverest at delivering or stealing those same things (Book 1, 333e–334a). If the just man is a clever guardian of idle money, he must also be a clever thief of it, meaning justice is a certain art of stealing for the benefit of friends and the harm of enemies (Book 1, 334a–b).
