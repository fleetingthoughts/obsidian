In 476a, Socrates starts to lay the foundation for his theory of forms and in 477a, he defines terms that relate the philosopher to the forms. What definitions does he create to define our capacity to understand them
#### Notes
When Socrates creates the dichotomy of the forms (e.g. Beauty and Ugly; Justice and Injustice). To know about these is to know what "is". When he defines ignorance as knowing nothing, he is referring to the absence and knowing nothing. I do not think Socrates is saying that knowing Ugly or Injustice is the same as ignorance.
