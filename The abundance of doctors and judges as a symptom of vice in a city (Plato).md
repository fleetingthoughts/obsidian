What does a high demand for prominent judges and doctors reveal about the educational state of a city?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: A proper education in simple music and gymnastics produces moderate souls and healthy bodies (Book 3, 404e–405a).<br>• _Argument_: Because a healthy state has no natural need for imported justice or complex medical treatments, it is argued that a high public demand for judges and doctors is a definitive sign of a corrupt and base education, revealing a deep physical and psychological rot caused by idleness and lack of inner discipline (Book 3, 405a–d).
