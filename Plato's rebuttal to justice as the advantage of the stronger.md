What is the initial logical contradiction Socrates exposes in Thrasymachus's definition of justice as the advantage of the stronger?
#### Notes
Thrasymachus agrees that (1) it is just to obey the commands of the rulers, and (2) rulers are fallible and sometimes command laws that are actually disadvantageous to themselves (Book 1, 339b–c). Therefore, when rulers make mistakes and command disadvantageous laws, the ruled, in obeying, are doing what is disadvantageous to the stronger, meaning the advantage of the stronger is no more just than the disadvantage (Book 1, 339c–e).
