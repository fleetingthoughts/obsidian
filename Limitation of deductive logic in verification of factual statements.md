What is the logical basis for Ayer's assertion that pure deductive logic operating solely on immediate sense-experience cannot yield knowledge of unobserved events or external reality?
#### Notes
**Refutations**: Causal relations are not logical (denials involve no contradiction), not analytically derived from finite experiences, and do not contain unobservable "necessary connections."<br>**Definition**: The assertion "C causes E" is equivalent to the universal synthetic hypothesis "whenever C occurs, E follows" across an indefinite range of instances.<br>**Ayer's Correction**: Hume's psychological definition fails because asserting "C caused E" without prior observed constant conjunction is not self-contradictory. Causality is empirical regular association, not logical necessity. _(Chapter 2, pp. 53–55)_
