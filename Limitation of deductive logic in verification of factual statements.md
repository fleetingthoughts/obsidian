What is the logical basis for Ayer's assertion that pure deductive logic operating solely on immediate sense-experience cannot yield knowledge of unobserved events or external reality?
#### Notes
**Nature of Propositions**: Deductive Limit & Humean Event Independence.<br><br>**Premise 1**: A valid deductive inference cannot assert anything in its conclusion that is not already implicitly contained within its premises.<br>**Premise 2 (Humean Principle)**: No single observed empirical event or immediate sense-datum intrinsically points to, or logically entails, the existence of any distinct unobserved past, present, or future event.<br>**Unstated Assumption**: Inferences from observed sense-data to unobserved events rely entirely on general synthetic rules or causal laws.<br>**Conclusion**: Synthetic general rules cannot be derived by pure formal deduction from immediate experiences; they must be established inductively. Consequently, pure deduction operating on immediate data cannot advance a single step beyond present experience.<br>**Citation**: Chapter 2, Pages 47–48.
