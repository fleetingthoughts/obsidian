Let $A$ be an $m \times n$ matrix and $B$ be an $n \times p$ matrix. The columns and rows of the resulting product $AB$ can be understood as explicit linear combinations of the constituent matrices.
#### Notes
Let $u_j$ and $v_j$ denote the $j$-th columns of $AB$ and $B$, respectively. Then $u_j = Av_j$
