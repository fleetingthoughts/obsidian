Let $A$ be an $m \times n$ matrix and $B$ be an $n \times p$ matrix. The columns and rows of the resulting product $AB$ can be understood as explicit linear combinations of the constituent matrices.
#### Notes
Column $j$ of $AB$ is a linear combination of the columns of $A$, with coefficients drawn from column $j$ of $B$. Symmetrically, row $i$ of $AB$ is a linear combination of the rows of $B$, with coefficients drawn from row $i$ of $A$.
