What is the formal logical structure of hypothesis falsification, and why is it considered deductively valid?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Formal Logical Schema.** The logical structure is Modus Tollens: "If H is true, then so is I; I is shown to be false; therefore, H is false." This is deductively valid because if the premises are true, the conclusion is unfailingly true; a single verified false implication is logically sufficient to reject the hypothesis. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 2, p. 6
