In confirmation theory, why does the incremental confirmation provided by a single new favorable instance of a hypothesis exhibit diminishing returns?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Epistemological Argument.** The increase in confirmation effected by one new favorable instance generally becomes smaller as the number of previously established favorable instances grows. If thousands of confirmatory cases of the same kind are already available, the addition of one more favorable finding raises the confirmation but little, illustrating the diminishing epistemic utility of pure repetition. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 4, p. 33)
