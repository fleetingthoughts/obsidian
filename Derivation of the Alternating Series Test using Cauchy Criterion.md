Derive the alternating series test using the Cauchy Criterion
#### Notes
1. Express the difference of partial sums for $m > n$: $\vert{}s_m - s_n\vert{} = \vert{}a_{n+1} - a_{n+2} + a_{n+3} - \dots \pm a_m\vert{}$.
2. Group adjacent terms and apply the monotonic decrease hypothesis to establish $0 \le \vert{}s_m - s_n\vert{} \le a_{n+1}$.
3. Apply the limit hypothesis ($\lim_{n \to \infty} a_n = 0$) to bound $a_{n+1} < \epsilon$ for sufficiently large $N$.
4. Conclude the partial sums form a Cauchy sequence, proving series convergence.
