How does the historical discovery of the planet Neptune illustrate a Deductive-Nomological explanation where the crucial step is discovering a particular fact rather than a new law?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Historical Case Example.** To explain irregularities in the motion of Uranus, Leverrier and Adams did not invent new laws; they used existing Newtonian laws of gravitation and motion. By conjoining these laws with the hypothesized particular fact of an undetected outer planet (calculating its mass, position, and orbit), they deductively derived the observed orbital deviations of Uranus. The explanation was confirmed when Neptune was discovered at the predicted location. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 5, p. 5)
