What is the logical relationship between causal explanations and universal laws in Hempel's Deductive-Nomological framework?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Epistemological Premise.** Every causal statement asserting that a particular event of kind $G$ was caused by an event of kind $F$ presupposes corresponding general laws. This is because the classical causal maxim "same cause, same effect" logically implies the universal claim that whenever an event of kind $F$ occurs, it is always and without exception accompanied by an event of kind $G$. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 5, p. 53
