Prove: For any $A \in \mathcal{M}_{m \times n}(F)$, $x \in F^n$, and $y \in F^m$, $\langle Ax, y \rangle_m = \langle x, A^* y \rangle_n$.
#### Notes
Using the matrix representation of the standard inner product, $\langle Ax, y \rangle_m = y^*(Ax) = (y^* A)x = (A^* y)^* x = \langle x, A^* y \rangle_n$.
