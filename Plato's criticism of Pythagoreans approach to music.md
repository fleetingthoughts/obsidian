What is Socrates' critique of the Pythagorean and popular approaches to harmonics?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Measuring heard accords and sounds puts "ears before the intelligence" and produces a labor without profit (Book 7, 531a–b).<br>• _Argument_: Because popular musicians merely torture strings on pegs, and even the Pythagoreans limit their search to finding numbers in heard chords, it is argued that both fail to rise to the level of problems to investigate which numbers are concordant and which are not, and why (Book 7, 531b–c). This makes harmonics useless unless it is used to search for the fair and the good (Book 7, 531c).
