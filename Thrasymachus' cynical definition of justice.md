After Socrates rebuts Polemarchus' definition of justice, Thrasymachus enters the stage and his argument can be broken down into 3 phases:
- How does Plato narratively treat Thrasymachus? What is he compared to?
- What is the initial definition of justice that Socrates makes?
- Following the defusal of his first definition? How does Thrasymachus amend his proposition of justice? How does Socrates rebut this definition as well with respect to conventional definitions of virtue and ruling as a virtue
- What final amendment does Thrasymachus make?
- Socrates defuses this final amendment in two parts:
	- How does he first challenge the amendment Thrasymachus makes and what definition does he accuse him of sneakily modifying that he addresses first
	- What is the rather shaky argument he makes against Thrasymachus' final amendment?
