List five standard $T$-invariant subspaces of a finite-dimensional vector space $V$ for a linear operator $T$.
#### Notes
$\{0\}$, $V$, $N(T)$, $R(T)$, and $E_\lambda$ (for any eigenvalue $\lambda$).
