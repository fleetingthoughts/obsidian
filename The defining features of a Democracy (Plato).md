What are the defining features of the Democratic state, and why is it called the "many-colored cloak"?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Democracy prioritizes absolute freedom, free speech, and the license to do whatever one wants.<br>• _Argument_: Because of this total license, each citizen organizes their life privately exactly as pleases them, producing all sorts of diverse human characters. It is argued that democracy is like a "many-colored cloak decorated in all hues," representing the fairest of regimes to the uneducated eye, functioning as a "general store" of constitutions where any individual can choose whatever pattern of government they like because there is no compulsion to rule or be ruled (Book 8, 557b–e).
