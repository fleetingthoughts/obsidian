What are the internal and bridge principles involved in the kinetic theory's explanation of Boyle's law $P \propto 1/V$?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Historical Case Example.** The internal principles are the assumptions characterizing microphenomena at the molecular level, including the random motion of molecules and the probabilistic laws governing them. The explanation requires two bridge principles: (1) that macroscopic gas pressure is quantitatively equal to the average momentum delivered per second by molecular impacts per unit area of the container walls, and (2) that the mean kinetic energy of the molecules remains constant as long as the macroscopic temperature is constant. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 6, pp. 72-73)
