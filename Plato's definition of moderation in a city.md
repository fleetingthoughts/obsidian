How does Socrates define Civic Moderation (_Sophrosyne_), and how does its structural location differ from Wisdom and Courage?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Moderation is defined as a certain order, harmony, and mastery over physical pleasures and desires, traditionally called being "stronger than oneself" (Book 4, 430e-431b).<br>• _Argument_: Unlike wisdom and courage, which reside in specialized, isolated parts of the city, it is argued that moderation stretches throughout the entire scale from top to bottom, bringing the rulers, the warriors, and the producers into a harmonious agreement that the calculating part of the state must rule (Book 4, 431c-432a).
