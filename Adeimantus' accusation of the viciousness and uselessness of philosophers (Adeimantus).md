What is the dialectical challenge Adeimantus raises against Socrates' claim that the city should be turned over to philosophers?
#### Notes
**Premise.** Adeimantus challenges Socrates by pointing out that while listeners cannot refute Socrates' step-by-step dialectic, empirical observation ("in deed") reveals that those who pursue philosophy past youth do not become ideal rulers, but instead either become completely vicious or, even if they remain decent, become entirely useless to their cities (Book 6, 487b–d).
