What are the educational premises stated by Socrates in a Guardian's education and what does that necessitate with the curation of their education? What aspect of society does Socrates specifically target with respect to a child's education?
#### Notes
**Premises on Education**
- **Two Branches:** Gymnastic for bodies, music (including speeches/stories) for the soul (376e).
- **Sequence:** Education in music/speeches precedes gymnastics (376e–377a).
- **Plasticity of the Young:** The young and tender stage is most plastic, assimilating itself to whatever model stamp is applied (377a–b).
Because of these things, we must be careful with the way we conduct stories: Stories must be censored:

**Policy / Rules of Censorship**
- **Supervision of Tale-Makers:** Founders must approve fine tales and reject unapproved ones (377b–c).
- **Rejection of Bad Lies:** Poets (Hesiod, Homer) make "bad representations" of what gods and heroes are like, like a painter painting something that doesn't resemble the subject (377d–e).
- **Specific Censored Myths:** Stories of Uranus being castrated by Cronos, Cronos taking revenge, or gods warring and plotting against each other must not be spoken in the city—even if made with a hidden sense—because young minds cannot distinguish hidden sense from literal meaning (377e–378e).
