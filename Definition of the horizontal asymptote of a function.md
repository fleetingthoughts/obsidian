Construct the rigorous definition for a functional limit as $x$ approaches infinity: $\lim_{x\to \infty} f(x) = L$.
#### Notes
For all $\epsilon > 0$, there exists an $M > 0$ such that whenever $x > M$, it follows that $\vert{}f(x) - L\vert{} < \epsilon$.
