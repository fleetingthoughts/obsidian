If $W$ is a $T$-invariant subspace of $V$, then $W$ is $g(T)$-invariant for any polynomial $g(t) \in P(F)$.
#### Notes
Since $W$ is closed under $T$, it is closed under $T^k$ for any $k \ge 0$. Linear combinations of powers of $T$ map $W$ into $W$, making $W$ invariant under $g(T)$.
