How does Hempel define the **systematic import** of a scientific concept, and what metaphor does he use to represent it?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** Hempel uses the metaphor of **knots in a network**, where scientific concepts are the knots and laws or theoretical principles are the "nomic threads" connecting them. The systematic import of a conceptual knot is determined by its strength or position in this network—specifically, how many nomic threads converge upon or issue from it to enhance the simplicity, explanatory power, and systematic unification of the theory. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 7, p. 94)
