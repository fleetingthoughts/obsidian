Define uniform convergence of a sequence of functions $(f_n)$ to $f$ on a set $A \subseteq \mathbf{R}$.
#### Notes
For every $\epsilon > 0$, there exists an $N \in \mathbf{N}$ such that $\vert{}f_n(x) - f(x)\vert{} < \epsilon$ whenever $n \ge N$ and $x \in A$.
