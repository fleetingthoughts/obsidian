State the equivalent condition for $V=W_1\oplus W_2$ with respect to the representation of $v\in V$ in terms of vectors in $W_1$ and $W_2$.
#### Notes
Let $V$ be a vector space and $W_1, W_2$ be subspaces of $V$. The vector space $V$ is the direct sum of $W_1$ and $W_2$ ($V = W_1 \oplus W_2$) if and only if every vector $v \in V$ can be uniquely expressed in the form $v = w_1 + w_2$, where $w_1 \in W_1$ and $w_2 \in W_2$.
