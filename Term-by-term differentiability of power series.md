State the term-by-term differentiability theorem for power series
#### Notes
If $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n x^n$ converges for all $x \in (-R, R)$, then the differentiated series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} n a_n x^{n-1}$ converges at each $x \in (-R, R)$, with uniform convergence on compact sets contained in $(-R, R)$.
