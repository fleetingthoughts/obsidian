How is the "autonomy of biology" defined in contrast to mechanism within Hempel's reductionist framework
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** The autonomy of biology is defined as the logical denial of the reducibility of biology to physics and chemistry. It asserts that biological concepts cannot be fully defined using only physico-chemical terms, and that biological laws cannot be fully derived from physico-chemical principles, meaning biology possesses autonomous concepts and laws that transcend those of the physical sciences. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 8, p. 102)
