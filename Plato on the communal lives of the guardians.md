How must guardians live to prevent them from becoming "savage masters" (416a)?
#### Notes
Prohibition of private property and the handling of silver/gold currency is the only way to preserve the guardians' "divine" soul-metal from mortal pollution (416d–417a).

- **Key Propositions/Claims:**
    - No private property; open houses; common mess; fixed installments as wages (416d–417a).
    - If guardians possess private land and currency, they become "householders and farmers" instead of guardians (417a).
- **Assumed Premises:**
    - The possession of currency leads inevitably to "hating and being hated" (417b).
- **Argued Premises:**
    - The "Shepherd and Dog" analogy (416a): If the guardians become "moneymakers," they become the city's "enemies within" (417b), ensuring its destruction.
- **Connective Tissue:** These laws constitute the final safeguard, ensuring that the internal educational tuning is reinforced by the material conditions of the _Kallipolis_.
