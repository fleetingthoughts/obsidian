How does the multiplication of private tyrannic characters in a city logically lead to the establishment of a political tyrant?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: A city's regime is a macro-projection of the dominant characters of its citizens (Book 9, 575c–d).<br>• _Argument_: When private individuals with tyrannic souls become numerous in a city and realize their collective strength, they exploit the folly of the democratic populace. It is argued that they select the single individual among them who has the biggest, most extreme internal tyrant ruling his own soul, and set him up as the political tyrant over the entire state, using force to enslave the fatherland if it does not submit willingly (Book 9, 575c–e).
