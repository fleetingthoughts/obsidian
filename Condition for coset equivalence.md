State the condition for two cosets $v_1 + W$ and $v_2 + W$ to be equal.
#### Notes
Let $V$ be a vector space and $W$ be a subspace of $V$. Two cosets $v_1 + W$ and $v_2 + W$ are equal ($v_1 + W = v_2 + W$) if and only if:

$$v_1 - v_2 \in W$$

_(Equivalently, $v_2 - v_1 \in W$.)_
