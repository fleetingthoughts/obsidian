State the sequential criterion for a function $f : A \to \mathbf{R}$ to fail to be uniformly continuous.
#### Notes
There exists $\epsilon_0 > 0$ and sequences $(x_n), (y_n) \subseteq A$ such that $\lim \vert{}x_n - y_n\vert{} = 0$ while $\vert{}f(x_n) - f(y_n)\vert{} \ge \epsilon_0$ for all $n \in \mathbf{N}$.
