Define a Simple Closed Oriented Curve
#### Notes
A curve represented by continuous functions $x=\phi(t), y=\psi(t)$ on $[a,b]$ with $\phi(a)=\phi(b)$ and $\psi(a)=\psi(b)$, such that different parameter values in the half-open interval $[a,b)$ map to different points on the curve.
