In the final movement of Book I, how does Socrates define the _ergon_ (work) and _arete_ (virtue) of the human soul?
#### Notes
The specific work (ergon) of the soul is living, managing, and deliberating (353d). The proper virtue (arete) required to perform that work well is justice, and its vice is injustice (353e).
