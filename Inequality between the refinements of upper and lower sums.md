Let $f: [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$ be bounded and let $P \subseteq Q$ be partitions of $[a, b]$. State the inequality relating the upper and lower sums of $P$ and $Q$.
#### Notes
$L(f, P) \le L(f, Q) \le U(f, Q) \le U(f, P)$.
