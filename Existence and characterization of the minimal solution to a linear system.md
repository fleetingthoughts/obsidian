State the theorem characterizing the minimal solution to a consistent linear system $Ax = b$ where $A \in \mathcal{M}_{m \times n}(F)$ and $b \in F^m$.
#### Notes
There exists a unique minimal solution $s$ to $Ax = b$, and $s$ is the unique solution to $Ax = b$ that lies in $R(L_{A^*})$; specifically, if $(AA^*)u = b$, then $s = A^* u$.
