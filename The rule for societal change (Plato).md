What is the universal, foundational rule Socrates establishes regarding how political regimes undergo constitutional transformation?
#### Notes
**Premise.** Socrates establishes the starting sociopolitical premise that constitutional change in every regime always originates from the ruling class itself when faction (_stasis_) arises within it; if the ruling class remains of one mind, the regime cannot be moved (Book 8, 545d).
