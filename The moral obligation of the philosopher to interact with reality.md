The deductive argument proving it is just to force the enlightened philosophers to abandon continuous contemplation and return to the cave to rule.
#### Notes
- **Premise:** The law does not exist to make one specific class exceptionally happy, but to harmonize and bind the entire city together for the common benefit (519e).
    
- **Pivot:** Unlike philosophers in corrupt cities who grow up spontaneously and owe no debt, the philosophers of the just city were bred and educated by the commonwealth like "leaders in hives" (520a-b).
    
- **Conclusion:** Because they owe a debt for their rearing, it is strictly just to compel them to descend into the cave and rule as a necessary duty, thereby preventing the factional wars caused by men hungering for power (520b-d).
