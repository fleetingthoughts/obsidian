How does the logical role of experimentation as a "method of test" differ from its role as a "method of discovery"?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Methodological Distinction.** As a method of test, experimentation evaluates an antecedently proposed hypothesis. As a method of discovery, experimentation acts as a heuristic device to guide the formulation of a new hypothesis. In discovery, keeping all but one suspected relevant factor constant is a valid strategy to systematically isolate and mathematically define the dependent variable's functional relationship to individual variables. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 3, pp. 21-22)
