State the boundary conditions and constraints required for the interior extremum theorem to guarantee $f'(c) = 0$.
#### Notes
The domain must be an open interval $(a, b)$, $f$ must be differentiable on $(a, b)$, and the extremum $c$ must be an interior point of the interval.
