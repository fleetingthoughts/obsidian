How does Socrates establish the hierarchy of the soul over the body, and how does this affect the decision to escape?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition**: Deductive Argument. **Philosophical Concept**: Mind-Body Dualism / Moral Perfectionism. **Inferential Logic**: Socrates argues that: (1) if the body is ruined by bad physical advice, life is not worth living; (2) the part of us concerned with justice and injustice (the soul) is far more valuable than the body; (3) therefore, if the soul is ruined by unjust actions, life is even less worth living. Because escaping prison unjustly would corrupt the soul, death is preferable to living with a corrupted soul. **Unstated Assumption**: Unjust actions cause objective, irreversible harm to the soul, regardless of whether they are detected or punished. **Citation**: _Crito_, 47e–48b [Passage 5].
