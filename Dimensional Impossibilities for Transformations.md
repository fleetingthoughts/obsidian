Let $V$ and $W$ be finite-dimensional vector spaces and $T: V \rightarrow W$ be a linear transformation. The relative dimensions of $V$ and $W$ dictate strict structural impossibilities regarding whether $T$ can be one-to-one or onto.
#### Notes
Let $V$ and $W$ be finite-dimensional vector spaces. If $\dim(V) < \dim(W)$, then $T$ cannot be onto.

Let $V$ and $W$ be finite-dimensional vector spaces. If $\dim(V) > \dim(W)$, then $T$ cannot be one-to-one.
