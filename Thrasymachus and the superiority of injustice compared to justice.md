According to Thrasymachus, why is "perfect injustice" (tyranny) superior to justice?
#### Notes
He argues that the just man always gets less than the unjust man in contracts, taxes, and public offices (Book 1, 343d–344a). Tyranny represents perfect injustice because it takes away others' property all at once by stealth and force (Book 1, 344a–b). While partial wrongdoers are punished and shamed, the tyrant is called happy and blessed, proving that injustice on a sufficient scale is mightier, freer, and more masterful than justice (Book 1, 344b–c).
