How is the oligarchical youth created?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: A youth's values are transformed when they witness the traumatic political ruin of their parents.<br>• _Argument_: When a son sees his timocratic father fail in office, suffer at the hands of sycophants, and lose his entire property through death, exile, or dishonor, the son is frightened. He thrusts love of honor and spiritedness headlong off the throne of his soul, turning greedily to saving and money-making. It is argued that he makes the money-loving part the great king of his mind, enslaving the calculating and spirited parts to sit on the ground as slaves, calculating only how to make money and praising only the wealthy (Book 8, 553a–d).
