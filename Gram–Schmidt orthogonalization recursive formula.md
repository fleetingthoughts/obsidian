Let $\{w_1, w_2, \dots, w_n\}$ be a linearly independent subset of an inner product space $V$. State the recursive formula for the orthogonal set $\{v_1, v_2, \dots, v_n\}$ generated by the Gram–Schmidt process.
#### Notes
$v_1 = w_1$ and

$$v_k = w_k - \sum_{j=1}^{k-1} \frac{\langle w_k, v_j \rangle}{\Vert{}v_j\Vert{}^2} v_j \quad \text{for } 2 \le k \le n.$$
