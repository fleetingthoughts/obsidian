What is the primary defining mechanism of the Democratic regime?
#### Notes
**Premise.** Socrates establishes the definition that a Democracy comes into being when the poor win, execute or exile their rivals, and share the regime and ruling offices with the remaining citizens on an equal basis, distributing offices for the most part by lot (Book 8, 557a).
