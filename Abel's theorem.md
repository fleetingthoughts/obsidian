State the Abel lemma for bounding partial sums.
#### Notes
Let $(b_n)$ satisfy $b_1 \ge b_2 \ge b_3 \ge \dots \ge 0$, and let $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n$ be a series whose partial sums are bounded by $A$. Then for all $n \in \mathbf{N}$, $\vert{}\sum_{k=1}^n a_k b_k\vert{} \le A b_1$.
