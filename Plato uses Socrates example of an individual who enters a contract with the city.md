How do the Laws of Athens demonstrate that Socrates gave "tacit consent" to their authority, and why is this agreement considered "just"?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition**: Empirical Premise / Social Contract Argument. **Philosophical Concept**: Tacit Consent / Voluntary Social Contract. **Inferential Logic**: The Laws show that: (1) Socrates chose to remain in Athens for 70 years, leaving the city less than even the lame, blind, or handicapped, and fathering his children there; (2) he had the freedom to leave with his property at any point to go to other Greek or foreign cities if he was dissatisfied; (3) he did not choose exile at his trial when he had the legal opportunity. Therefore, Socrates' active, long-term, and uncoerced residence constitutes a voluntary, tacit agreement to live under and obey Athenian law. **Citation**: _Crito_, 51d–53a [Passage 8, 9].
