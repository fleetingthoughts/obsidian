Define uniform continuity for a function $f : A \to \mathbf{R}$.
#### Notes
For every $\epsilon > 0$ there exists a $\delta > 0$ such that for all $x, y \in A$, $\vert{}x - y\vert{} < \delta$ implies $\vert{}f(x) - f(y)\vert{} < \epsilon$.
