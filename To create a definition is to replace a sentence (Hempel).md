What is the logical consequence of a formal definition in terms of translatability, and how does Willard Van Orman Quine summarize this concept?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Formal Logical Rule.** Any term introduced by an analytic or stipulative definition can be completely eliminated from a sentence by substituting its _definiens_ for the _definiendum_, turning the sentence into an equivalent one that no longer contains the term (e.g., translating statements about density into statements about mass and volume). As Quine summarizes, "to define a term is to show how to avoid it." (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 7, p. 86)
