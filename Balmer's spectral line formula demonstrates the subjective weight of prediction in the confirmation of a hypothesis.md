How does J. J. Balmer's 1885 spectral line formula illustrate the distinction between fitting existing data and predicting novel test implications?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Historical Case Example.** Balmer constructed his mathematical formula for the wavelengths of hydrogen gas spectra using only four lines measured by Ångström. He then used the formula to predict the wavelengths of lines yet to be measured or found. The subsequent experimental discovery of 35 consecutive lines matching his predictions provided a powerful example of confirmation by novel, "new" test implications. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 4, p. 37)
