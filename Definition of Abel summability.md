Define what it means for a series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n$ to be Abel-summable to $L$
#### Notes
The power series $f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n x^n$ converges for all $x \in [0, 1)$ and $\lim_{x \to 1^-} f(x) = L$.
