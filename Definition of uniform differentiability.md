Define uniform differentiability for a function $f : A \to \mathbf{R}$.
#### Notes
Given $\epsilon > 0$, there exists a $\delta > 0$ such that $\left\vert{} \frac{f(x) - f(y)}{x - y} - f'(y) \right\vert{} < \epsilon$ whenever $0 < \vert{}x - y\vert{} < \delta$ with $x, y \in A$.
