Address the objection that men and women have distinct natures and must therefore have distinct societal functions. Articulate the logical pivot Socrates uses involving the "bald and longhaired shoemakers" to defend gender equality.
#### Notes
Socrates distinguishes between eristic (arguing over words) and dialectic (analyzing forms) (453e–454a). A difference in nature only mandates a difference in pursuit if the difference is strictly relevant to the _art_ or _practice_ itself (454b–c). Just as bald and longhaired men can both be shoemakers, the physical difference in procreation (bearing vs. mounting) does not dictate a difference in the cognitive or spiritual capacity to guard or rule (454d–455d).
