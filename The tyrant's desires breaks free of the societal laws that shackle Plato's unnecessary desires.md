How does the fully developed tyrannic soul permanently overthrow the moral and legal constraints of childhood that shackle their unnecessary desires?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Childhood opinions about fine, base, and just things are held in place by the law and the father during a democratic regime in the soul (Book 9, 574d–e).<br>• _Argument_: Once love establishes an absolute internal tyranny, the lawless desires that were formerly released only as temporary dreams during sleep become the individual's continuous, waking reality. Love lives within him in total anarchy, leading him to commit every terrible murder, sacrilege, and lawless deed to feed itself and its rowdy cohort, permanently conquering his childhood moral opinions (Book 9, 574d–575a).
