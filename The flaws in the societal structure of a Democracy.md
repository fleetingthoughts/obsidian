What are the three classes of Democracy, and how do they interact to spark civil faction?
#### Notes
**Premise.** Socrates establishes that democracy is divided into three parts: (1) **The Drones**: the idle, extravagant class that leads, speaks, and acts in the assembly; (2) **The Rich**: the most orderly by nature, who accumulate the most wealth and serve as the "drones' pasture"; and (3) **The People**: the agrarian, non-meddling class with small means, who are the most numerous and sovereign when they assemble, but will only assemble if they receive a share of the "honey" (Book 8, 564c–565a).
