What is the epistemic definition of Civic Wisdom (_Sophia_) in the Kallipolis, and in which class does it reside?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Wisdom is a specific kind of knowledge that enables good counsel, which is not concerned with particular crafts like carpentry or bronze-making, but with the entire city's internal and external relations (Book 4, 428b-c).<br>• _Argument_: Because this comprehensive political knowledge is possessed exclusively by the perfect guardians, it is argued that the city is wise as a whole because of its smallest ruling class and the wisdom residing within them (Book 4, 428d-429a).
