How does the Myth of Er shift the moral responsibility of a soul's destiny entirely onto the individual chooser rather than the gods? (Book 10, 617d–e)
#### Notes
**Premise.** In the Myth of Er, the spokesman of Lachesis (daughter of Necessity) lays out the patterns of lives and declares: **"A demon will not select you, but you will choose a demon... Virtue is without a master... The blame belongs to him who chooses; god is blameless"** (617d–e). This establishes the foundational metaphysical principle of individual agency and accountability: a soul's cosmic fate is not pre-determined by divine whim, but is the direct, voluntary consequence of its own choice of life (Book 10, 617d–e).
