The deductive argument distinguishing dialectic as the ultimate "coping stone" of education, structurally superior to all mathematical arts.
#### Notes
- **Premise:** The mathematical arts (geometry, astronomy) only dream about what is, because they rely on unquestioned hypotheses and cannot give a true account of their own beginnings (533b-c).
    
- **Pivot:** The dialectical method is the only inquiry that systematically destroys its own hypotheses, using them strictly to pull the soul's eye out of the "barbaric bog" and ascend to a secure, unhypothetical beginning (533c-d).
    
- **Conclusion:** Therefore, only dialectic can grasp the pure essence of each thing and the Good itself without the use of the senses, completing the intellectual journey (532a-b, 534e).
