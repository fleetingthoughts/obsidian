What is the psychological structure of the Democratic man?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: The democratic man lives under the "law of equality," treating all pleasures as having equal value.<br>• _Argument_: Because he refuses to admit true speech stating that some pleasures are noble and others are bad, he shakes his head and declares all are alike. It is argued that he lives day by day, handing over internal rule by lot to whichever desire happens along: one day drinking and playing the flute, the next dieting and exercising, sometimes idling, sometimes doing philosophy, and frequently jumping into politics to do whatever chances to come to him (Book 8, 561b–e).
