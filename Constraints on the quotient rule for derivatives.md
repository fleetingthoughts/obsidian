State the strict boundary constraints required for the quotient rule $(f/g)'(c) = \frac{g(c)f'(c) - f(c)g'(c)}{[g(c)]^2}$ to hold.
#### Notes
$f$ and $g$ must be defined on an interval $A$, both must be differentiable at $c \in A$, and $g(c) \neq 0$.
