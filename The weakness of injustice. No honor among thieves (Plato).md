What is the "band of robbers" argument Socrates uses to prove that injustice is inherently weak and factional?
#### Notes
Socrates argues that injustice produces factions, hatreds, and internal conflicts, whereas justice produces unanimity and friendship (Book 1, 351d). Even a band of robbers, pirates, or any group engaged in a common unjust enterprise cannot accomplish anything if they act unjustly toward one another, because mutual injustice breeds internal warfare that renders them unable to act in common (Book 1, 351d–352a).
