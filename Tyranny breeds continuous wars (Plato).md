Why is the Tyrant logically compelled to stir up continuous wars as his primary domestic policy?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: The tyrant must keep the citizens in a constant state of dependence and poverty to prevent them from plotting rebellion.<br>• _Argument_: In his first days, the tyrant smiles, promises land, and pretends to be gentle. However, once reconciled with or having destroyed his external enemies, it is argued that he must constantly set some war in motion so that the people will remain in absolute need of a leader, and because contributing money to the war will make them too poor and busy to plot against him (Book 8, 566e–567a).
