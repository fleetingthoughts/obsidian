Why does Socrates reject poetic passages that praise abundance of food, meat, and wine?
#### Notes
Such depictions elevate physical gratification and hedonism as the ultimate "good," which undermines the self-mastery and temperance required for the guardians' education (Book 3, 389e–390b).
