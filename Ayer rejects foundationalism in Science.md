Why does Ayer argue that attempting to build an _a priori_ deductive system out of purely analytic tautologies fails to produce a genuine system of knowledge about the universe?
#### Notes
**Nature of Propositions**: Logical Limit of Analytic Propositions.<br><br>**Premise 1**: An _a priori_ truth is an analytic tautology, which records linguistic conventions and contains no factual information about the empirical world.<br>**Premise 2**: From a set of tautologies, only further tautologies can be validly derived by formal deduction.<br>**Conclusion**: A system composed strictly of tautologies cannot yield factual conclusions about the universe. Therefore, attempting to derive factual knowledge from _a priori_ first principles is logically impossible.<br>**Citation**: Chapter 2, Page 49.
