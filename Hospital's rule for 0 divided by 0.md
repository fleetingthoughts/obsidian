State L'Hospital's rule for the 0/0 indeterminate form at a limit point $a$.
#### Notes
Let $f$ and $g$ be continuous on an interval containing $a$, and differentiable except possibly at $a$. If $f(a) = g(a) = 0$ and $g'(x) \ne 0$ for $x \ne a$, then $\lim_{x \to a} \frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)} = L \implies \lim_{x \to a} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = L$.
