What is the epistemic definition of Civic Courage (_Andreia_), and how does Socrates illustrate its permanence?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Courage is a power of preservation produced by law through education regarding what things are terrible and of what sort (Book 4, 429c).<br>• _Argument_: Because the guardians have been prepared by music and gymnastics to receive the laws like a permanent, colorfast dye, it is argued that true civic courage is the preservation of this lawful opinion through everything, resisting being washed away by the powerful "lyes" of pleasure, pain, fear, and desire (Book 4, 429d-430c).
