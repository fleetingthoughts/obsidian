Why are the requirements of explanatory relevance and testability logically interrelated, and what is the direction of this implication?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Logical Premise.** The two requirements are asymmetrical: any proposed explanation that meets the requirement of explanatory relevance also automatically meets the requirement of testability. This is because if an explanans provides grounds for expecting an explanandum, either deductively or inductively, it must imply that explanandum under specified test conditions, which inherently yields test implications. However, the converse does not hold, as a set of statements can be empirically testable without explaining why a phenomenon occurs. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 5, p. 49 / p. 240)
