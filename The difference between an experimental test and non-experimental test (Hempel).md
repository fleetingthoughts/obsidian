What is the methodological distinction between an experimental test and a non-experimental test of a hypothesis?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Methodological Distinction.** An experimental test is possible when the test conditions (C) can be technologically realized and actively controlled at will by the scientist. A non-experimental test must be employed when active control is impossible, requiring the scientist to seek out or wait for nature to spontaneously realize the necessary conditions. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 2, pp. 7–8)
