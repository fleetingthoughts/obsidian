Given two linear transformations $U: W \to Z$ and $T:V \to W$, if $UT$ is surjective, what can we say about $U$ or $T$
#### Notes
Let $V, W, Z$ be vector spaces, and let $T: V \to W$ and $U: W \to Z$ be linear transformations. If the composition $UT$ is onto (surjective), then the outer transformation $U$ must be onto. The inner transformation $T$ need not be onto.
