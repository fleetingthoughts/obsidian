Let $c_0, c_1, \dots, c_n$ be $n+1$ distinct scalars in an infinite field $F$. The set of associated Lagrange polynomials $\beta = \{f_0, f_1, \dots, f_n\}$ constitutes a basis for $P_n(F)$. Furthermore, for any $n+1$ scalars $b_0, b_1, \dots, b_n$, the unique polynomial $g \in P_n(F)$ satisfying $g(c_j) = b_j$ for all $j$ is given by $g(x) = \sum_{i=0}^n b_i f_i(x)$
