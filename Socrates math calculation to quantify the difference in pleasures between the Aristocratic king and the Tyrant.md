What is the mathematical calculation Socrates uses to quantify the difference in pleasure between the Kingly and Tyrannic lives?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: The tyrant is removed from the kingly man by a set number of constitutional steps (Book 9, 587c–d).<br>• _Argument_: Counting Aristocracy/Kingship, Timocracy, Oligarchy, Democracy, and Tyranny, the oligarch is third from the king, and the tyrant is third from the oligarch, making the tyrant's distance of separation a length of 3 × 3 = 9. By transforming this linear distance into a plane and then a cube to represent the real depth of their lives, Socrates multiplies 9 × 9 × 9 (9 cubed) to yield **729**. He concludes that the king lives 729 times more pleasantly than the tyrant, and the tyrant lives more disagreeably by this exact same distance (Book 9, 587d–588a).
