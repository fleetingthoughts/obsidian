What is the primary epistemological purpose of a scientific theory, and how does it accomplish this beyond empirical laws?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** Theories seek to explain previously discovered empirical uniformities and afford a deeper, more accurate understanding of phenomena. They accomplish this by construing macroscopic occurrences as manifestations of underlying entities and processes that lie behind or beneath them, which are assumed to be governed by characteristic theoretical laws. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 6, p. 70)
