#### Notes
## Definition of a subsequence

Let $(a_n)$ be a sequence. A subsequence $(a_{n_k})$ is formed by selecting terms from $(a_n)$ using a strictly increasing sequence of natural numbers $n_1 < n_2 < n_3 < \dots$ as indices. Therefore, the $k$-th term of the subsequence is the $n_k$-th term of the original sequence.
