As a result of his position on the proper style of speeches, what does Socrates say about the place of poets and craftsmen who have a tangential skills to poets in imitation like painters, architects and sculptors
#### Notes
Because the ideal city is founded on the Principle of Specialization ("one man, one job"), there is no "double" or "manifold" man allowed; a versatile imitator violates this civic harmony, so he is honored as sacred but escorted out of the city (Book 3, 397c–398b).

Non-poetic craftsman must be supervised to prevent them from depicting bad, licentious, or graceless images in their work, so that guardians are not exposed to images of vice daily ("cropping on bad grass") until they gradually construct a large moral corruption within their souls (Book 3, 401a–c).
