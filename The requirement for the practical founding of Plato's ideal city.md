What radical claim 
#### Notes
***People over the age of 10 must be exiled (540d-541b).*** This is the ultimate, radical climax of Socrates's political project. After spending seven books building a theoretical utopia, this is his exact answer for how it can actually be brought into existence. To institute the regime quickly and easily, the philosopher-kings must exile everyone older than ten out into the country, taking over only the children to rear them in the new laws. Without this final policy, the city remains a purely theoretical thought experiment. This step is practically required to wipe away the corrupt dispositions of the parents and make the ideal city a reality.
