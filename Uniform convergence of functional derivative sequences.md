Let $(f_n)$ be a sequence of differentiable functions defined on $[a, b]$. State the operational $\epsilon$-$N$ definition for $(f_n')$ to converge uniformly to a function $g$ on $[a, b]$.
#### Notes
For every $\epsilon \gt 0$, there exists $N \in \mathbf{N}$ such that $\vert{}f_n'(x) - g(x)\vert{} \lt \epsilon$ for all $n \ge N$ and all $x \in [a, b]$.
