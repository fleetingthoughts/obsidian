What are the dimensions of $\mathcal{L}(V,W)$ if $dim(V)=n$ and $dim(W)=m$.
#### Notes
If $V$ and $W$ are finite-dimensional vector spaces of dimensions $n$ and $m$ respectively, then $L(V, W)$ is finite-dimensional with dimension $mn$.
