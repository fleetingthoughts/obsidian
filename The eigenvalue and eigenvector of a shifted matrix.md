Prove if $x$ is an eigenvector of $T$ with eigenvalue $\lambda$, then $x$ is an eigenvector of $U = T - cI$ with eigenvalue $\lambda - c$.
#### Notes
$U(x) = (T - cI)(x) = T(x) - cx = \lambda x - cx = (\lambda - c)x$.
