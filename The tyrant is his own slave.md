Why is the tyrannic character argued to exist in a permanent state of existential isolation and slavery?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Freedom and friendship require a baseline of equality, trust, and mutual respect (Book 9, 575e–576a).<br>• _Argument_: Because a tyrannic nature is always seeking mastery, its relations with others are strictly asymmetric: it either cringes as a flatterer to get what it needs or acts as an alien master once it succeeds. Consequently, it is argued that they live their entire lives without ever being friends with anyone, permanently trapped in a master-slave dynamic where they are either one man's master or another's slave, never tasting true freedom or friendship (Book 9, 575e–576a).
