Prove: If $(f_n)$ is a sequence of differentiable functions on $[a, b]$ such that both $(f_n)$ and $(f_n')$ converge uniformly on $[a, b]$, then $f = \lim f_n$ cannot fail to be differentiable at any point in $[a, b]$.
#### Notes
Uniform convergence of $(f_n)$ to $f$ implies pointwise convergence on $[a, b]$. Combined with uniform convergence of $(f_n')$, the differentiable limit theorem guarantees that $f$ is differentiable at every point in $[a, b]$.
