Show the Bolzano-Weierstrass Theorem implies the Monotone Convergence theorem without using the Axiom of Completeness.
#### Notes
- By BW, the bounded sequence has a convergent subsequence yielding a limit $L$.
    
- Because the original sequence is monotone, the convergence of its subsequence mathematically forces the entire original sequence to converge to that exact same limit $L$.
