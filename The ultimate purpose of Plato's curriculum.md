What is the ultimate purpose of the mathematical curriculum (the quadrivium) for the future dialectician? How do these studies differ from dialectics
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: All the preparatory mathematical studies (calculation, geometry, stereometry, astronomy, harmonics) are merely a prelude to the "song" of dialectic (Book 7, 531d).<br>• _Argument_: It is argued that these studies are useful only if they are brought to a unified overview that reveals their mutual community, kinship, and relationship to one another and the nature of Being; this ability to grasp the overview is the primary test of a dialectical nature (Book 7, 531c–d, 537c).

These studies only prepare for dialectics and so they differ from it:

**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Mathematical sciences (like geometry) dream about what is but cannot see it clearly because they treat their starting assumptions as unquestioned "hypotheses" (Book 7, 533b–c).<br>• _Argument_: Because a system built on unknown starting assumptions can never become true knowledge, it is argued that only the dialectical method is capable of destroying these starting hypotheses, ascending to the unhypothetical beginning of the whole (the Good) to make it secure, and then descending to conclusions using only Forms, without any reliance on sensory images (Book 7, 533c–d).
