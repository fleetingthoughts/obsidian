State the conditions under which the product of two uniformly convergent sequences of functions converges uniformly.
#### Notes
If $f_n \to f$ uniformly and $g_n \to g$ uniformly on $A$, and there exists $M > 0$ such that $\vert{}f_n(x)\vert{} \le M$ and $\vert{}g_n(x)\vert{} \le M$ for all $n \in \mathbf{N}$ and $x \in A$, then $f_n g_n \to fg$ uniformly on $A$.
