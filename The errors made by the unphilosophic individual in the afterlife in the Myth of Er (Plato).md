What dangerous cognitive error is exposed by the first soul who chooses a life in the Myth of Er, and why does it occur? (Book 10, 619b–d)
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: The soul that draws the first lot immediately and greedily chooses the greatest tyranny due to folly and gluttony, failing to notice that he is fated to eat his own children (619b–c).<br>• _Argument_: This soul had previously come from heaven, having lived in an orderly regime in his former life, where he participated in virtue **by habit, without philosophy** (619c–d). Because his virtue was merely external conformity rather than rational, dialectical understanding of the Good, he was unpracticed in intellectual labors and failed to evaluate the consequences of his choice. Therefore, it is argued that mere habitual virtue is dangerously unstable, and only active philosophical study saves the soul from moral catastrophe across lifetimes (Book 10, 619c–e).
