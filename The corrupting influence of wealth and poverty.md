Why must the guardians actively prevent both extreme wealth and extreme poverty from creeping into the city?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Both wealth and poverty act as corrupting forces that destroy a craftsman's capacity to practice their specialized art (Book 4, 421d).<br>• _Argument_: Because wealth produces luxury, idleness, and a desire for political innovation, while poverty produces illiberality, shoddy workmanship, and wrongdoing, it is argued that guardians must forbid both economic extremes to prevent the deterioration of the city's essential crafts and stability (Book 4, 421d-422a).
