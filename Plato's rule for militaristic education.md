In his 2nd wave of arguments, Socrates makes concrete recommendations for the military that will enforce all the values of a well-governed city. Walk through recommendation for a militaristic education and his views on the risk.

#### Notes
- ***The militaristic education and contingencies*** A militaristic education should begin from childhood and children should be allowed to participate and observe which as the additional benefit and encouraging soldiers to fight more earnestly with people watching (466e–467a). When Glaucon challenges the dangers of a child on the battlefield, Socrates asserts the premise that some risks are worth taking and as a contingency, children should observe on horseback so that they can flee at a moments notice (**467e**)
- ***Military Honors.*** Soldiers who prove themselves can kiss anyone (468b–468c) and they will be given posthumous honors as if they were the gold class and their tombs forever maintained (468e–469b).
- ***Court martials.*** Acts of cowardice and desertion are punished by demotion to the lower class of farmers and artisans (468a)
