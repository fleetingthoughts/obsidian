Prove if an $m \times n$ matrix $A$ has rank $m$, there exists an $n \times m$ matrix $B$ such that $AB = I_m$.
#### Notes
$\text{rank}(A) = m$ implies $L_A: F^n \to F^m$ is surjective. Thus, $L_A$ admits a right inverse transformation $T$. Letting $B$ be the matrix representation of $T$ yields $AB = I_m$.
