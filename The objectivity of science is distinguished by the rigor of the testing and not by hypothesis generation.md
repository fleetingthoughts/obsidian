If scientific hypotheses are freely invented using creative imagination (context of discovery), how is scientific objectivity preserved (context of justification)?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Epistemological Premise.** Objectivity is safeguarded by the requirement that hypotheses, no matter how they are invented, must be validated through rigorous, public empirical testing. They can only be accepted into the body of scientific knowledge if they successfully pass critical scrutiny, including the checking of their derived test implications against careful observation or experiment. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 2, p. 15
