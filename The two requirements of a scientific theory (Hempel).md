What are the definitions of, and distinction between, internal principles and bridge principles in the formulation of a scientific theory?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** Internal principles characterize the basic entities and processes invoked by a theory (which are often unobservable) and the laws to which they conform. Bridge principles indicate how these theoretical processes and entities are related to empirical, macroscopic phenomena with which we are already acquainted, enabling the theory to explain, predict, or retrodict observable occurrences. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 6, p. 72)
