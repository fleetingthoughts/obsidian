State the hypotheses required for the inverse of a function $f : [a, b] \to \mathbf{R}$ to be differentiable.
#### Notes
$f$ must be continuous, one-to-one, and differentiable on $[a, b]$ with $f'(x) \neq 0$ for all $x \in [a, b]$.
