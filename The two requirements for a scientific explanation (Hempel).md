What are the two systematic requirements that any scientific explanation must satisfy, and how are they defined?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** The first is the **requirement of explanatory relevance**, which dictates that the explanatory information must afford good logical grounds for believing that the phenomenon to be explained (explanandum) did indeed occur. The second is the **requirement of testability**, which dictates that the statements constituting the explanans must be capable of empirical test, meaning they must logically yield test implications. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 5, p. 48)
