Let $V$ be a vector space generated by a set $G$ containing exactly $n$ vectors, and let $L$ be a linearly independent subset of $V$ containing exactly $m$ vectors. Then $m \le n$, and there exists a subset $H \subseteq G$ containing exactly $n - m$ vectors such that $L \cup H$ generates $V$.
