State the endpoint integrability theorem for a function $f : [a, b] \to \mathbf{R}$ that is integrable on $[c, b]$ for every $c \in (a, b)$.
#### Notes
If $f$ is bounded on $[a, b]$ and integrable on $[c, b]$ for all $c \in (a, b)$, then $f$ is Riemann-integrable on $[a, b]$.
