Let $T: V \to W$ be a linear transformation between finite-dimensional inner product spaces with orthonormal bases $\beta$ for $V$ and $\gamma$ for $W$. How is $[T^*]_\gamma^\beta$ related to $[T]_\beta^\gamma$?
#### Notes
$$[T^*]_\gamma^\beta = ([T]_\beta^\gamma)^*$$
