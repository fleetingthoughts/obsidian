What are the three equivalent statements for a subset $K \subseteq \mathbb{R}$ under the Heine-Borel Theorem?
#### Notes
$K$ is compact if any of the 3 condition are met:
- $K$ is compact (every open cover of $K$ has a finite subcover).
- $K$ is closed and bounded.
- $K$ is sequentially compact (every sequence in $K$ has a subsequence that converges to a point in $K$).
