Why must $f: [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$ be bounded to define $L(f, P)$ and $U(f, P)$?
#### Notes
Boundedness guarantees by the completeness of $\mathbb{R}$ that $m_k = \inf\{f(x) : x \in [x_{k-1}, x_k]\}$ and $M_k = \sup\{f(x) : x \in [x_{k-1}, x_k]\}$ exist as finite real numbers on every subinterval.
