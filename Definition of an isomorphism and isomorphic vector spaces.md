Define an isomorphism and what it means for two vector spaces to be isomorphic
#### Notes
A linear transformation $T: V \to W$ is an isomorphism if it is one-to-one and onto.

Two vector spaces $V$ and $W$ are isomorphic (denoted $V \cong W$) if there exists an isomorphism $T: V \to W$.
