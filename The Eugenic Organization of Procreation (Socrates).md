Outline the mechanisms and deceptions the rulers must employ to ensure the guardian flock remains of the most eminent quality without causing internal faction.
#### Notes
Rulers must use "subtle lots" (rigged lotteries) to secretly pair the best men with the best women as often as possible, while the ordinary mate with the ordinary (459d–460a). Children of the good are reared in anonymous pens by nurses, while those of the worse are hidden away in an unseen place (460b–c). Strict age limits for authorized procreation are enforced: ages 20–40 for women, and peak-speed to age 55 for men (460e–461a).
