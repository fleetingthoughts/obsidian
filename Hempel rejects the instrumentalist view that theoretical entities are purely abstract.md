How does the instrumentalist use the underdetermination of theories by data to deny the existence of theoretical entities, and what is Hempel's counter-argument?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Epistemological Critique.** The instrumentalist argues that because any finite set of empirical data can be fit by multiple competing theories (such as particle vs. wave theories of light), granting reality to the entities of one theory would force us to grant reality to those of another, meaning none can exist. Hempel counters that competing theories have further, differentiating implications that can be tested to eliminate rivals. While we can never narrow the field to just one theory with absolute certainty, this is a pervasive characteristic of all empirical knowledge, not a unique flaw of theoretical entities. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 6, pp. 79-81)
