Let $V$ be a vector space and $T: V \rightarrow V$ be a linear transformation. A transformation is algebraically idempotent (meaning $T = T^2$) if and only if it performs what specific geometric action on the vector space?
#### Notes
$T = T^2$ if and only if $T$ is a projection on the subspace $W_1 = \{y : T(y) = y\}$ along the null space $N(T)$.
