Regarding power and capability, what does Thrasymachus claim is the ultimate result of perfect injustice on a massive scale (tyranny)?
#### Notes
He claims that injustice, when it comes into being on a sufficient scale, is mightier, freer, and more masterful than justice, making the perpetrator happy and blessed (344a–c).
