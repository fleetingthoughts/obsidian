Why does Socrates forbid portraying gods or noteworthy men as being overcome by laughter?
#### Notes
Excessive laughter represents a violent psychological shift that compromises self-control and emotional stability; portraying figures of authority in this state invites a corresponding lack of restraint in those who imitate them (Book 3, 388e–389a).
