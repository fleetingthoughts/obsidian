State the Cauchy criterion for uniform convergence of a series of functions $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f_n$ on $A \subseteq \mathbf{R}$.
#### Notes
The series converges uniformly on $A$ if and only if for every $\epsilon > 0$ there exists an $N \in \mathbf{N}$ such that $\vert{}f_{m+1}(x) + f_{m+2}(x) + \dots + f_n(x)\vert{} < \epsilon$ whenever $n > m \ge N$ and $x \in A$.
