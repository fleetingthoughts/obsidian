What is the semantic distinction between the "explanandum" and the "explanans" in Hempel's model of explanation?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** The explanandum is the phenomenon to be accounted for, described by the "explanandum sentence" $E$. The explanans is the explanatory information itself, specified by the "explanans sentences" $L_i$ and $C_j$, which jointly provide the deductive or inductive grounds for expecting the explanandum. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 5, p. 50)
