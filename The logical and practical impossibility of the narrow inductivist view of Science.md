Why does Hempel argue that Stage 1 of the narrow inductivist model (collecting "all" facts without prior hypotheses) is a physical and logical impossibility?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Epistemological Argument.** A complete collection of "all facts up to now" is impossible because there is an infinite number and variety of facts (e.g., recording the shape of every grain of sand on Earth, the brand of our writing pen, our passing thoughts). A scientist cannot observe everything, and thus must select which facts to observe. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 2, pp. 11–12)
