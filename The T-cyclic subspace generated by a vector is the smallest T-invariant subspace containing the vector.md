What is the relationship between the $T$-cyclic subspace generated by $v$ and other $T$-invariant subspaces containing $v$?
#### Notes
The $T$-cyclic subspace generated by $v$ is the smallest $T$-invariant subspace containing $v$. Any $T$-invariant subspace containing $v$ must contain the $T$-cyclic subspace.
