Let $W$ be a subspace of a finite-dimensional inner product space $V$. State the identity relating $\dim(V)$, $\dim(W)$, and $\dim(W^\perp)$.
#### Notes
$$\dim(V) = \dim(W) + \dim(W^\perp).$$
