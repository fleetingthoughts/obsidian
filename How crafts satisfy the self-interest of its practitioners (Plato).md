Why must wages be attached to crafts, and what are the three types of wages Socrates identifies?
#### Notes
Because no art or kind of rule provides for its own benefit but only for the benefit of the weaker ruled class, no one chooses to rule voluntarily without receiving compensation (Book 1, 345e–346e). Socrates identifies the three types of wages that must be offered to make men willing to rule as: (1) money, (2) honor, or (3) a penalty for refusing to rule (Book 1, 347a).
