What is the core argument of the "Carpenter vs. Rich Man" comparison regarding the social value of life?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Every citizen in a well-governed city has a specific, necessary job to perform that justifies their existence (Book 3, 406c).<br>• _Argument_: Socrates argues that while a carpenter has a clear work to do (meaning a life spent treating disease instead of working is useless), the rich man is treated by society as if he has no function; however, because practicing virtue is everyone's required function, and because excessive medical pampering prevents attention to virtue, a sickly life spent in constant medical care is socially useless for both rich and poor (Book 3, 406c–407c).
