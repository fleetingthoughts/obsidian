After abandoning the guarding argument, what cultural definition of justice (inherited from Simonides) does Polemarchus fall back on?
#### Notes
Polemarchus asserts that justice is treating people according to what is owed to them: doing good to friends (if they are good) and harm to enemies (if they are bad) (332a–d, 335a)
