When Cephalus implies justice is "speaking the truth and giving back what one takes," how does Socrates use the concept of _conditional harm_ to logically break this absolute standard?
#### Notes
Socrates introduces the counter-example of a friend who deposits weapons but goes mad (331c). Returning the weapons fulfills the condition of "giving back," but results in conditional harm, proving it cannot be a universal standard for justice (331c).
