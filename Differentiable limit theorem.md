State the differentiable limit theorem for a sequence of functions $(f_n)$ on $[a, b]$ that converges pointwise to $f$.
#### Notes
If each $f_n$ is differentiable on $[a, b]$ and $(f_n')$ converges uniformly on $[a, b]$ to a function $g$, then $f$ is differentiable on $[a, b]$ and $f' = g$.
