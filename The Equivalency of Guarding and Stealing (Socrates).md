When Polemarchus claims justice is the art of guarding useless deposits, what exact deductive step does Socrates use to equate the just man with a thief?
#### Notes
Socrates establishes that the capacity to perfectly guard a thing logically mirrors the capacity to steal it (e.g., a clever guardian of an army can steal enemy plans) (333e–334a). Thus, if the just man is the ultimate guardian of money, he must possess the exact skill set of a clever thief (334a–334b).
