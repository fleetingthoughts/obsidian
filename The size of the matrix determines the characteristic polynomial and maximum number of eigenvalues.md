If $A$ is a $n\times n$ matrix, what do we know about the degree of its characteristic polynomial and the number of eigenvalues it can possibly have?
#### Notes
**Theorem 5.3**: Let $A \in M_{n \times n}(F)$.
- (a) The characteristic polynomial of $A$ is a polynomial of degree $n$ with leading coefficient $(-1)^n$.
- (b) $A$ has at most $n$ distinct eigenvalues.
