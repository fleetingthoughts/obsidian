How does the tripartite division of the soul generate three distinct classes of human beings, and what are their respective pleasures?
#### Notes
**Premise.** Socrates establishes that corresponding to the three parts of the soul, there are three distinct pleasures, desires, and ruling principles that define the three primary classes of human beings: (1) **The Wisdom-loving/Learning-loving class**, whose pleasure is directed toward knowing the truth as it is; (2) **The Victory-loving/Honor-loving class**, whose pleasure is set on mastery, victory, and reputation; and (3) **The Gain-loving/Money-loving class**, whose pleasure is derived from profit and material acquisition (Book 9, 580d–581c).
