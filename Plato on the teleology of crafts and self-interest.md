How does Socrates use the teleology of crafts (_techne_) in "precise speech" to refute the claim that rulers command their own self-interest?
#### Notes
He demonstrates that every craft is designed to provide what is advantageous for its weaker, ruled subject matter (e.g., medicine provides for the body, horsemanship for horses) rather than for itself, since the art itself has no defects or needs (Book 1, 341b–342d). Because the crafts rule and are masters of their subject matter, a craftsman or ruler in precise speech never commands his own advantage, but rather that of the weaker and ruled subject (Book 1, 342d–e).
