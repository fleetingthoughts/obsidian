Why does the Tyrant's political survival force him to perform a "reverse medical purge" of the city?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: The tyrant cannot rule securely if citizens with free thoughts, courage, great minds, or wealth are left alive.<br>• _Argument_: Because manliest companions will criticize his actions, the tyrant is forced to gradually do away with all of them. Unlike doctors who perform a healthy purge by removing the worst elements and leaving the best, it is argued that the tyrant must do the opposite: purge the city of its courageous, wise, and rich citizens, leaving him under the "blessed necessity" of either living with the bad and hated multitude or ceasing to live (Book 8, 567b–d).
