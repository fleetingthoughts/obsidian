The Republic can be read as a literary work with the themes interwoven and foreshadowed like a traditional story rather than a dry philosophical text:
- What is the opening line of the Republic.
- Who are the characters first introduced and what are they doing
- Who interrupts Socrates and walk through the conversation they have and how it foreshadows later themes.
- What is Socrates jokingly coerced into doing that sets the scene for the rest of the Republic?
