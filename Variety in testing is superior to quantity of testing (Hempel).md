Why does the variety of supporting evidence lend stronger confirmation to a scientific hypothesis than a large quantity of uniform evidence, and what is the underlying logical rationale?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Epistemological Argument.** The greater the variety of test implications checked, the higher the probability of exposing an unfavorable instance if the hypothesis is false. A uniform body of evidence only tests a narrow, specific sub-hypothesis, whereas diverse tests explore a wider range of potential failure modes, thereby severely testing the general claim and minimizing the threat of a false positive. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 4, pp. 34-35)
