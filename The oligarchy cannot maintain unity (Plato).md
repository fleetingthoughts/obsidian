Why is an Oligarchic city incapable of maintaining political unity?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: A unified state must share a common civic interest.<br>• _Argument_: Because oligarchy splits the state into two opposing factions based on wealth—the city of the poor and the city of the rich—dwelling together in the same place and constantly plotting against each other, it is argued that the state is of necessity not one, but two, destroying its ontological and civic unity (Book 8, 551d).
