Identify the logical fallacy in proving the Cayley-Hamilton theorem via $f(A) = \det(A - AI) = \det(O) = 0$.
#### Notes
The characteristic polynomial $f(t)$ is a scalar-valued function. Evaluating $f(A)$ maps polynomial operations onto matrices to yield the zero matrix $O$, whereas $\det(A - AI)$ improperly evaluates a matrix expression inside a scalar determinant.
