State the Riesz representation theorem for a linear functional $g: V \to F$ on a finite-dimensional inner product space $V$.
#### Notes
There exists a unique vector $y \in V$ such that:

$$g(x) = \langle x, y \rangle \quad \text{for all } x \in V$$
