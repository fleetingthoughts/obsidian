Define what it means for a function $f : A \to \mathbf{R}$ to be Lipschitz on $A$.
#### Notes
There exists an $M > 0$ such that $\left\vert{} \frac{f(x) - f(y)}{x - y} \right\vert{} \le M$ for all $x \ne y$ in $A$.
