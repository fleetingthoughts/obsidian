What is the formal logical schema of a deductive reduction of a biological law, and how do connecting laws function within it?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Formal Logical Model.** Let $P_1$ and $P_2$ be physico-chemical expressions, and let $B_1$ and $B_2$ be biological expressions. Given a physico-chemical law $L_P$: "All $P_1$ are $P_2$" and the connecting laws "All $B_1$ are $P_1$" ($P_1$ is necessary for $B_1$) and "All $P_2$ are $B_2$" ($P_2$ is sufficient for $B_2$), we can deductively derive the biological law $L_B$: "All $B_1$ are $B_2$". This shows that the derivability of biological laws depends entirely on the empirical establishment of suitable connecting laws. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 8, p. 104)
