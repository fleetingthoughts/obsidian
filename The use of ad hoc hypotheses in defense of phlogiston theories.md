How did defenders of the horror vacui and phlogiston theories historically employ ad hoc hypotheses to resist falsification?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Historical Case Example.** To explain why mercury columns are shorter at high altitudes, defenders of horror vacui assumed nature's abhorrence of a vacuum decreases with altitude. To explain why metals gain weight when calcinated, defenders of phlogiston theory assumed phlogiston has "negative weight" so its escape increases remaining mass. Neither hypothesis led to independent test implications. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 3, pp. 28-30)
