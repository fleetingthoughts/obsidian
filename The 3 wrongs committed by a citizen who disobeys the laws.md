According to the Laws, what are the three distinct moral wrongs committed by a citizen who disobeys and escapes?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition**: Argument / Conclusion. **Philosophical Concept**: Multi-Source Obligation (Gratitude, Filial Duty, and Consent). **Inferential Logic**: Disobeying the state's verdict is a three-fold wrong because the escaping citizen: (1) disobeys their moral parents (filial impiety); (2) disobeys those who nurtured and educated them (ingratitude); and (3) breaks a voluntary agreement to obey (breach of contract), while failing to either persuade the state or show how it did wrong. **Citation**: _Crito_, 51e–52a [Passage 8].
