Given a vector space $V$ and a subspace $W$, state the definition of the coset of $W$ containing $v \in V$.
#### Notes
Let $V$ be a vector space over a field and $W$ be a subspace of $V$. For a given vector $v \in V$, the **coset** of $W$ containing $v$, typically denoted as $v + W$, is defined as the set of all vectors obtained by adding $v$ to every element of $W$:
<!--SR:!fsrs,2026-10-03T22:53:29.607Z,8,8.2956,1,2,1,0,0,2026-09-25T22:53:29.607Z-->

$$v + W = \{v + w \mid w \in W\}$$

(Note: Because the operation of vector addition is commutative within a vector space, left cosets and right cosets are identical, meaning $v + W = W + v$. Therefore, it is simply referred to as "the coset" rather than specifying left or right).
