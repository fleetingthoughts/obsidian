What is the logical consequence of confirmation holism (the Duhem-Quine thesis) when a test implication is empirically falsified?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Logical Argument.** When a test implication I is shown to be false, we use the Modus Tollens schema to infer that the conjunction of the core hypothesis and its auxiliary assumptions is false: not(H & A), which simplifies to (not H or not A). This means we cannot deductively isolate and reject the core hypothesis H alone; we can only infer that either the hypothesis H or at least one of the auxiliary assumptions A is false. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 3, pp. 22-23)
