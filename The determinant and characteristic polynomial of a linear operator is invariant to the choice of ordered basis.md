State and prove the relationship between the determinant and characteristic polynomial of a linear operator with two different ordered basis.
#### Notes
For a linear operator $T$ on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$, both $\det(T)$ and $f(t) = \det([T]_\beta - tI_n)$ are independent of the choice of ordered basis $\beta$.
