Define the conjugate transpose (adjoint) $A^*$ of an $m \times n$ matrix $A \in \mathcal{M}_{m \times n}(F)$.
#### Notes
The $n \times m$ matrix $A^* \in \mathcal{M}_{n \times m}(F)$ with entries:

$$(A^*)_{ij} = \overline{A_{ji}} \quad \text{for all } 1 \le i \le n, \, 1 \le j \le m$$
