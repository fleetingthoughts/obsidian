Define the eigenspace $E_\lambda$ of a linear operator $T$ on a vector space $V$ corresponding to an eigenvalue $\lambda$.
#### Notes
$E_\lambda = \{x \in V : T(x) = \lambda x\} = N(T - \lambda I_V)$.
