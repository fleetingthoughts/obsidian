State the structural mandate Socrates proposes regarding the living arrangements, marriages, and child-rearing of the guardian class and the analogy he makes with respect to a specific interlocutor
#### Notes
All women and children are to be held in common, with no private marriages or households (457c–d). Parents will not know their own offspring, and children will not know their biological parents (457d)
