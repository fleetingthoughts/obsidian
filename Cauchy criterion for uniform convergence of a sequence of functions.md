State the Cauchy criterion for uniform convergence of a sequence of functions $(f_n)$ on $A \subseteq \mathbf{R}$.
#### Notes
$(f_n)$ converges uniformly on $A$ if and only if for every $\epsilon > 0$ there exists an $N \in \mathbf{N}$ such that $\vert{}f_n(x) - f_m(x)\vert{} < \epsilon$ whenever $m, n \ge N$ and $x \in A$.
