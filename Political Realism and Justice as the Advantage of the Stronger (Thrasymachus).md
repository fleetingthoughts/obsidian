What is Thrasymachus's  initial definition of justice regarding cities and their political rulers?
#### Notes
Thrasymachus defines justice as "nothing other than the advantage of the stronger" (Book 1, 338c). He argues that the established ruling group in any city (whether democratic, tyrannical, or aristocratic) is the master and sets down laws strictly for its own advantage, declaring this advantage to be "just" for the ruled and punishing anyone who departs from it (Book 8, 338d–339a).
