What are the primary natural virtues of the philosophic soul established by Socrates in the opening of Book VI?
#### Notes
**Premise.** Socrates establishes the starting essential traits of the philosophic nature: (1) an erotic love for learning that discloses unchanging Being (485a–b); (2) absolute love of truth and hatred of falsehood (485c–d); (3) moderation and non-covetousness, as their desires are channeled entirely toward the soul (485d–e); (4) magnificence and the contemplation of all time and being, which makes death seem untrying (486a–b); (5) ease of learning and a strong memory (486c–d); and (6) a well-proportioned, musical, and measured nature (486d–e) (Book 6, 485a–486e).
