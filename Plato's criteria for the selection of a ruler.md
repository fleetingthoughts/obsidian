How are the rulers to be selected and the city’s unity preserved (412c)?
#### Notes
**Assumptions:**
- We need a an overseer of the city who is best suited to guarding the city(412 b)
- The older should rule the younger
**Key Propositions/Claims:**
- Selection via testing with "terrors and pleasures" to find who is "hard to bewitch" (413d–e).
- The "well-tuned" soul becomes the primary criterion for selecting the city’s rulers.
- Because the guardians protect the city, we must pick the best guardian to serve as the ruler, and so we require contests of music and gymnastics.
