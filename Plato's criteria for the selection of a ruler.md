What are the primary criteria for selecting the rulers from the guardian class?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: The rulers must be those who are older, more powerful, prudent, and deeply loving of the city (Book 3, 412b–d).<br>• _Argument_: Because a person loves most that which they believe shares their own self-interest, it is argued that they must select guardians who have demonstrated throughout their lives a steadfast, unyielding conviction that they must only do what is advantageous to the city (Book 3, 412c–e).
