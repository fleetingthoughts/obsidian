Prove: Let $T$ be a linear operator on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$, and let $f(t)$ be the characteristic polynomial of $T$. Then $f(T) = T_0$.
#### Notes
- Evaluate $f(T)(v)$ for an arbitrary $v \in V$; trivially $0$ if $v = 0$.
    
- For $v \neq 0$, let $W$ be the $T$-cyclic subspace generated by $v$.
    
- Identify $g(T)(v) = 0$, where $g(t)$ is the characteristic polynomial of $T_W$, utilizing the companion matrix characteristic polynomial.
    
- Use the property that $g(t)$ divides $f(t)$ to write $f(t) = q(t)g(t)$.
    
- Compute $f(T)(v) = q(T)(g(T)(v)) = q(T)(0) = 0$, proving $f(T) = T_0$.
