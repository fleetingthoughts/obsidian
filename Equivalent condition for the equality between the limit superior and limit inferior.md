State the equivalence condition for the limit superior to be equal to the limit inferior using the convergence of the original sequence.
#### Notes
- **Construction:** You must first extract a bounded, monotone subsequence from the original bounded sequence.
    
- **The Archimedean Pivot:** To use CC, you must prove this monotone subsequence is Cauchy. The Archimedean Property is implicitly required to prove that a bounded sequence cannot jump by $\epsilon$ infinitely many times without eventually exceeding its bounds.
