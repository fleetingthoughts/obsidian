If vector space $V$ has a dimension $n$, prove that any set of of $n$ independent vectors is a basis for $V$.
#### Notes
Let $V$ be a vector space with dimension $n$. Any generating set for $V$ containing exactly $n$ vectors is inherently a basis for $V$. Symmetrically, any linearly independent subset of $V$ containing exactly $n$ vectors is inherently a basis for $V$.
