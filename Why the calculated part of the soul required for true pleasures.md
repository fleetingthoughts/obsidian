Why are the pleasures of the spirited and desiring parts of the soul argued to be truest only when they follow the lead of the calculating part?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Justice in the soul is established when each internal part minds its own business and does not spark internal faction (Book 9, 586e).<br>• _Argument_: When the entire soul follows the wisdom-loving part, each of the three parts is able to mind its own business, act justly, and discover its own most appropriate, truest pleasures (Book 9, 586d–e). Conversely, if the spirited or desiring part takes control, it cannot find its own true pleasure and actively compels the other parts to pursue a false, alien pleasure, distorting the soul's harmony (Book 9, 587a).
