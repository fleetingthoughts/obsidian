State the term-by-term continuity theorem for a series of functions.
#### Notes
Let $f_n$ be continuous functions defined on a set $A \subseteq \mathbf{R}$. If $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f_n$ converges uniformly on $A$ to a function $f$, then $f$ is continuous on $A$.
