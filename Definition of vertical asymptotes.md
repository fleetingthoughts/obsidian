State the rigorous definition for an infinite functional limit: $\lim_{x\to c} f(x) = \infty$.
#### Notes
For all $M > 0$, there exists $\delta > 0$ such that whenever $0 < \vert{}x-c\vert{} < \delta$, it follows that $f(x) > M$.
