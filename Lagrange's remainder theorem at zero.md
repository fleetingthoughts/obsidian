State the conclusion of Lagrange's remainder theorem for $f$ centered at $0$ evaluated at $x \neq 0$ in $(-R, R)$.
#### Notes
There exists a point $c$ strictly between $0$ and $x$ (satisfying $\vert{}c\vert{} \lt \vert{}x\vert{}$) such that:

$$E_N(x) = \frac{f^{(N+1)}(c)}{(N+1)!} x^{N+1}$$
