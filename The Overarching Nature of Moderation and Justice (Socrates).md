Differentiate the structural function of Moderation and Justice from the localized nature of Wisdom and Courage.
#### Notes
Unlike Wisdom and Courage (which reside in specific parts), Moderation is an accord or harmony stretching across all classes, resulting in unanimous agreement on who should rule (430d–432a). Justice is the overarching power that allows the others to exist: it is defined as each class minding its own business and not meddling in the functions of the others (432b–434c).
