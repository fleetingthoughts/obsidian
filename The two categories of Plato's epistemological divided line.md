In Book 6, Socrates introduced the [the divided line analogy](Socrates'%20analogy%20of%20the%20divided%20line%20and%20its%20relation%20with%20Good.md) and calls out 4 segments. He then classifies these 4 areas into two [cognitive faculties](Socrates'%20initial%20conception%20of%20the%20forms%20and%20his%20definition%20of%20our%20cognitive%20faculties..md). What is this classification?
#### Notes
****The Naming of the Divided Line Segments (533d–534a)*** While Socrates introduced the Divided Line earlier, this is the exact moment he definitively locks in the terminology for Platonic epistemology. He categorizes the highest faculties as **knowledge** and **thought** (which together form **intellection**), and the lower faculties as **trust** and **imagination** (which together form **opinion**). He also definitively states the proportion: intellection relates to "being," while opinion relates to "coming into being". Grasping this specific vocabulary is absolutely essential for anyone taking a test on Plato, as this is the definitive framework for how he categorizes human cognition.
