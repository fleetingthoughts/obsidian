Define the radius of convergence for a power series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n x^n$.
#### Notes
There exists a value $R \in [0, \infty]$ such that the series converges for $\vert{}x\vert{} < R$ and diverges for $\vert{}x\vert{} > R$.
