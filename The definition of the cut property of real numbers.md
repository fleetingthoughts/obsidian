What is the formal statement of the Cut Property of the real numbers and its relation to the Axiom of Completeness
#### Notes
If $A$ and $B$ are nonempty, disjoint sets with $A \cup B = \mathbb{R}$ and $a < b$ for all $a \in A$ and $b \in B$, then there exists $c \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $x \le c$ whenever $x \in A$ and $x \ge c$ whenever $x \in B$.

The cut property and axiom of completeness are logically equivalent; either can be used as the fundamental axiom that distinguishes the real numbers from the rational numbers
