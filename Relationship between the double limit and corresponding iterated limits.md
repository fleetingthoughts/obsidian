If the double limit $\lim_{m,n\to\infty} a_{mn}$ exists, and both iterated limits $\lim_{n\to\infty} (\lim_{m\to\infty} a_{mn})$ and $\lim_{m\to\infty} (\lim_{n\to\infty} a_{mn})$ also exist, what is the relationship between the three limits? Is the existence of the iterated limits sufficient for the existence of the double limit? Is the existence of the double limit sufficient for the existence of the iterated limit?
#### Notes
 All three limits must be equal. The existence of the double limit does not imply the existence of any of the iterated limit or vice versa
