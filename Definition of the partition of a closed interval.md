Define a partition $P$ of a closed interval $[a, b]$
#### Notes
A finite set of points $P = \{x_0, x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n\} \subseteq [a, b]$ ordered such that $a = x_0 \lt x_1 \lt x_2 \lt \dots \lt x_n = b$.
