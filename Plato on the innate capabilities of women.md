What is the psychological premise Socrates establishes regarding the distribution of natural talents between men and women?
#### Notes
**Premise.** Socrates establishes that the natural aptitudes for all civic crafts, sciences, and virtues (such as medicine, music, gymnastics, war, and philosophy) are scattered alike among both male and female human beings, meaning there is no governing practice peculiar to a woman simply because she is a woman (Book 5, 455c–e).
