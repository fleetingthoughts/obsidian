How does the virtue of prudence (_phronesis_) differ ontologically from other bodily and moral virtues of the soul?
#### Notes
**Premise.** Socrates establishes the premise that while other virtues (like courage or moderation) are not present beforehand and are later produced by habits and physical exercise, the virtue of prudence belongs to a more divine element that never loses its inherent power, but depending on how it is turned, becomes either highly useful and helpful, or useless and harmful (Book 7, 518d–519a).
