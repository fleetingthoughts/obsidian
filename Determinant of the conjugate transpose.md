Prove: For any $A \in \mathcal{M}_{n \times n}(F)$, $\det(A^*) = \overline{\det(A)}$.
#### Notes
Since $A^* = \overline{A^t}$ and the determinant is a polynomial in the matrix entries that is invariant under transposition:

$$\det(A^*) = \det(\overline{A^t}) = \overline{\det(A^t)} = \overline{\det(A)}$$
