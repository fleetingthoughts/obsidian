State the formula for the coefficients $a_n$ of a power series $f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n x^n$ defined on a nontrivial open interval $(-R, R)$.
#### Notes
$$a_n = \frac{f^{(n)}(0)}{n!}$$
