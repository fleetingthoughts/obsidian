Explain the "statue-painting" analogy Socrates uses to justify the strict, unadorned lifestyle of the guardians.
#### Notes
Socrates argues that painting the eyes of a statue too beautifully ruins the proportion of the whole face (420c–d). Similarly, giving guardians conventional wealth ruins their function as guardians; the goal is the happiness of the entire city as a single unit, not the disproportionate happiness of one isolated class (420b, 421b–c).
