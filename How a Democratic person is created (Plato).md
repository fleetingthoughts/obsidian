How is a Democratic person created
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: A soul empty of noble studies and true arguments is highly vulnerable to cognitive invasion.<br>• _Argument_: When a stingy, uneducated oligarchic youth tastes the "drones' honey" (unnecessary pleasures) through clever, fiery beasts, his internal regime begins to shift. If his family scolds him, an internal faction arises. However, because his mind is empty of the best watchmen—fair studies and true speeches—the unnecessary desires eventually seize the acropolis of his soul. It is argued that they admit boasting, false opinions, which close the gates of his mind, rename vices as virtues (e.g., calling insolence "good education," anarchy "freedom," and shamelessness "courage"), and drive moderation and order over the frontier (Book 8, 559d–561a).
