Define what it means for two matrices $A, B \in M_{n \times n}(F)$ to be simultaneously diagonalizable.
#### Notes
There exists an invertible matrix $Q \in M_{n \times n}(F)$ such that both $Q^{-1} A Q$ and $Q^{-1} B Q$ are diagonal matrices.
