What is Socrates' "Analogy of the Physical Trainer," and what philosophical conclusion does it support regarding public opinion?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition**: Analogy / Premise. **Philosophical Concept**: Epistemic Elitism vs. Democratic Majoritarianism. **Inferential Logic**: Just as an athlete should only value the praise, blame, and advice of their trainer (the one who has knowledge) and ignore the opinion of the many (who lack expertise) to prevent physical ruin, a person must only value the judgment of the "one who understands justice and injustice" rather than the majority to prevent moral ruin of the soul. **Unstated Assumption**: Ethical inquiry and justice are objective domains of expertise (moral realism) analogous to medicine or physical training, rather than matters of subjective consensus or majoritarian preference. **Citation**: _Crito_, 47a–48b [Passage 4, 5].
