State the theorem regarding the uniform limit of Riemann-integrable functions on $[a, b]$.
#### Notes
If $(f_n)_{n=1}^\infty$ is a sequence of Riemann-integrable functions on $[a, b]$ and $f_n \to f$ uniformly on $[a, b]$, then $f$ is Riemann-integrable on $[a, b]$.
