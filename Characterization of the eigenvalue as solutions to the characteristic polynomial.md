State and prove an equivalent condition for $\lambda$ to be an eigenvalue to a matrix using the characteristic polynomial of a matrix
#### Notes
Let $A \in M_{n \times n}(F)$. Then a scalar $\lambda$ is an eigenvalue of $A$ if and only if $\det(A - \lambda I_n) = 0$.
