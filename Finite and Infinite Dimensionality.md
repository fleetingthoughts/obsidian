Define what it means for a vector space to be finite dimensional and what it means to be infinite dimensional.
#### Notes
A vector space $V$ is termed finite-dimensional if it possesses a basis consisting of a finite number of vectors. The unique integer $n$ denoting the number of elements in any basis for $V$ is defined as the dimension of $V$, denoted $\dim(V)$. If $V$ cannot be spanned by a finite set, it is defined as infinite-dimensional.
