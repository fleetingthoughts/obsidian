State the sufficient conditions for the rectifiability of a parametrically defined curve regarding its derivatives and the resulting formula.
#### Notes
The functions $x(t)$ and $y(t)$ have continuous derivatives for $a \le t \le b$. Formula: $L = \int_a^b \sqrt{\dot{x}^2 + \dot{y}^2} dt$.
