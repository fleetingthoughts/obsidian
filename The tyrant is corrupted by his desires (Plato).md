How does the dominant "tyrant Love" (_Eros_) within the youth systematically dismantle his moral psychology and exhaust his estate?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: The ruling desire (_Eros_) acts as an internal monarch or tyrant that pilots all other elements of the soul (Book 9, 573d).<br>• _Argument_: Because this insatiable love spawns a swarm of needy, intense desires every day and night, whatever revenues the youth has are rapidly exhausted, forcing him to borrow and strip his estate (Book 9, 573d–e). When resources fail, the desires, driven by the sting of love and acting as love's armed guards, compel him to seize the property of his elderly parents by deceit or force, even enslaving his necessary parents to unnecessary, newly-found companions (Book 9, 573e–574c).
