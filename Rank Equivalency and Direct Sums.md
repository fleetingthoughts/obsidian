Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional vector space, and let $T: V \rightarrow V$ be a linear transformation. If the rank of the transformation stabilizes immediately such that $\text{rank}(T) = \text{rank}(T^2)$, this forces a rigid internal separation within the vector space.text here
#### Notes
This condition implies that $R(T) \cap N(T) = \{0\}$, which structurally guarantees that the vector space decomposes into the direct sum $V = R(T) \oplus N(T)$.
