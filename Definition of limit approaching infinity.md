Define $\lim_{x \to c} g(x) = \infty$ for a function $g : A \to \mathbf{R}$ and a limit point $c$ of $A$. 
#### Notes
For every $M > 0$, there exists a $\delta > 0$ such that whenever $0 < \vert{}x - c\vert{} < \delta$, it follows that $g(x) \ge M$.
