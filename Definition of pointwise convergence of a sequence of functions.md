Define pointwise convergence of a sequence of functions $(f_n)$ to $f$ on a set $A \subseteq \mathbf{R}$.
#### Notes
For every $\epsilon > 0$ and $x \in A$, there exists an $N \in \mathbf{N}$ (dependent on $x$) such that $\vert{}f_n(x) - f(x)\vert{} < \epsilon$ whenever $n \ge N$.
