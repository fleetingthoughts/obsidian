Define the direct sum $B_1 \oplus B_2$ for square matrices $B_1 \in \mathcal{M}_{m \times m}(F)$ and $B_2 \in \mathcal{M}_{n \times n}(F)$.
#### Notes
The $(m+n) \times (m+n)$ block diagonal matrix $A$ with $B_1$ in the upper-left $m \times m$ block, $B_2$ in the lower-right $n \times n$ block, and zeros elsewhere.
