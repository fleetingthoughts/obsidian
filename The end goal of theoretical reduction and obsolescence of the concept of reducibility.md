Why does Hempel argue that the future unification of biology and physics might make the very concept of "reducibility" obsolete?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Methodological Argument.** In the course of future scientific research, the dividing lines between disciplines may become so blurred that a comprehensive, unified theory is couched in entirely novel terms that explain both biological and physical phenomena. In the vocabulary of such a comprehensive theory, the traditional division into "physico-chemical" and "specifically biological" terms would no longer be applicable, rendering the question of "reducing" one to the other logically meaningless. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 8, p. 105)
