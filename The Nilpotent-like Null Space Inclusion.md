Let $V$ be a vector space, and let $T: V \rightarrow V$ be linear. The composition of the transformation with itself evaluates to the zero transformation ($T^2 = T_0$) if and only if a specific subset inclusion holds between its range and null space.
#### Notes
$T^2 = T_0$ if and only if the entire range of $T$ is swallowed by its own null space, formally stated as $R(T) \subseteq N(T)$
