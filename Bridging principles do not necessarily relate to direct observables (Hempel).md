How does Bohr's explanation of Balmer's spectral formula demonstrate that bridge principles do not always connect theoretical entities directly to directly observable everyday things?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Epistemological Argument.** Bohr's bridge principles connect the unobservable quantum jumps of electrons between atomic energy levels with the wavelengths of emitted light, where (\lambda = \frac{hc}{\Delta E}). These wavelengths are not directly observable in the everyday sense and require highly indirect measurement procedures resting on other theories (like wave optics). Thus, bridge principles can link new theoretical terms to pretheoretical terms whose measurement and observation are already established and taken for granted by prior theories. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 6, p. 73)
