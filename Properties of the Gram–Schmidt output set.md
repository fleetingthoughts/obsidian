Let $S = \{w_1, w_2, \dots, w_n\}$ be a linearly independent subset of an inner product space $V$, and let $S' = \{v_1, v_2, \dots, v_n\}$ be constructed from $S$ via the Gram–Schmidt process. State the three structural properties of $S'$.
#### Notes
$S'$ is an orthogonal set, consists of nonzero vectors, and satisfies $\text{span}(S') = \text{span}(S)$.
