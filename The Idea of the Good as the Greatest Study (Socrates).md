State the ultimate epistemological target that Socrates defines as the "greatest study," and explain why the knowledge of justice and moderation is insufficient without it. What concession does Socrates make about his description of good
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: The Idea of the Good is that by which just things and other virtues become useful and beneficial to the soul (505a).<br>• _Argument_: Because possessing all things or having knowledge of all other things is of no profit if they are not good, it is argued that the Idea of the Good is the supreme object of study, and without knowledge of it, any guardian of the just and fair remains a blind, useless guide (Book 6, 505a–506a).
