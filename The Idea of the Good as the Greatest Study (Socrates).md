State the ultimate epistemological target that Socrates defines as the "greatest study," and explain why the knowledge of justice and moderation is insufficient without it. What concession does Socrates make about his description of good
#### Notes
- The "Idea of the Good" is the greatest study, as it is the very thing that makes justice and all other virtues useful and beneficial (505a). Possessing every other kind of knowledge or virtue is of no profit without knowing the Good, as it is impossible to be prudent about anything fine and good in the absence of the Good itself (505b).
