In confirmation theory, how does the formal logical derivation of a test implication demonstrate the necessity of auxiliary hypotheses?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Formal Logical Premise.** A test implication I is rarely derived deductively from a core hypothesis H alone. Its derivation almost always requires additional premises or auxiliary hypotheses A. Thus, the logical relationship is represented as the conditional statement: "If both H and A are true, then so is I" or ((H & A) -> I). (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 3, p. 22)
