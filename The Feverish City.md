What challenge does Glaucon make that results in a modification to what he calls the [The City of Sows](The%20City%20of%20Sows.md) as modelled by Socrates
#### Notes
Glaucon objects that Socrates has created a **"city of sows,"** lacking conventional comforts like couches, tables, relishes, and desserts

**The Second City: The Luxurious / Feverish City (372e–374a)**
**Argument on the Origin of War**
- **Premise:** Socrates calls the simple city the "healthy city," but agrees to examine a "feverish city" to see how justice and injustice grow (372e–373a).
- **Bloated Demands:** Couches, tables, relishes, perfumes, courtesans, painters, poets, servants, barbers, cooks, swineherds, and doctors are added (373a–d).
- **Origin of War:** The land that was sufficient to feed the original citizens becomes too small (373d). The city must cut off a piece of its neighbors' land, and neighbors will do the same if they abandon themselves to the unlimited acquisition of money beyond necessity (373d–e). War arises directly from factors whose presence produces private and public evils (373e–374a)
