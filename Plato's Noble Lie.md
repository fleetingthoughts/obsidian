State the story of the noble lie made by Socrates in Book 3 of the republic
#### Notes
he "Noble Lie" (Myth of the Metals) ensures that citizens accept their natural "admixture" and care for the city as a "mother" (414d–415c).

- **Key Propositions/Claims:**
    - Selection via testing with "terrors and pleasures" to find who is "hard to bewitch" (413d–e).
    - The Metals: Gold (Rulers), Silver (Auxiliaries), Iron/Bronze (Craftsmen) (415a).
- **Assumed Premises:**
    - The rulers must "love the city" most, believing its advantage is their own (412d).
- **Argued Premises:**
    - The "colts to noises" test (413d): Guardians must be tested against fear and pleasure to prove they will not "banish" the conviction to do what is best for the city.
- **Connective Tissue:** While the Myth is the theoretical solution, communal living in Part 9 is the empirical safeguard.
