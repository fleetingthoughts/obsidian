Why are ad hoc hypotheses scientifically objectionable if they are not logically inconsistent?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Methodological Argument.** Ad hoc hypotheses lack independent empirical import because they do not yield new test implications that allow for further testing. Furthermore, as more qualifying hypotheses are added to save a theory from cumulative adverse evidence, the total system becomes excessively complex, eventually collapsing when a simpler, more powerful alternative theory is proposed. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 3, pp. 29-30)
