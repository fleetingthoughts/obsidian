Define Euler's number as the limit of a sequence.
#### Notes
Euler's number ($e$) is defined mathematically as the limit of the sequence $a_n = \left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)^n$ as $n$ approaches infinity:
$$e = \lim_{n \to \infty} \left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)^n$$
