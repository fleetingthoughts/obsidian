Determine the equivalent condition for $v$ to be an eigenvector corresponding to a known eigenvalue $\lambda$
#### Notes
**Theorem 5.4**: Let $A \in M_{n \times n}(F)$, and let $\lambda$ be an eigenvalue of $A$. Vector $v \in F^n$ is an eigenvector of $A$ corresponding to $\lambda$ if and only if $v \neq 0$ and $(A - \lambda I)v = 0$.
