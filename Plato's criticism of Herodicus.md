How does Socrates critique the medical approach introduced by the gymnastic master Herodicus?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Medicine should quickly cure definite diseases so a person can return to their daily work (Book 3, 406c).<br>• _Argument_: Because Herodicus mixed gymnastic with medicine to treat his own chronic, incurable illness, he merely succeeded in prolonging his dying state into old age while neglecting his work and living in constant physical distress, which Socrates argues is a useless and harmful use of the medical art (Book 3, 406a–b).
