What is Hempel's core argument regarding the logical relationship between empirical facts and scientific hypotheses in terms of "relevance"?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Epistemological Argument.** Empirical findings can only be qualified as logically relevant or irrelevant in reference to a given _hypothesis_, not a given _problem_. A tentative answer (hypothesis) is required to determine what data are worth collecting; without a hypothesis, data-gathering is directionless and self-defeating. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 2, p. 12)
