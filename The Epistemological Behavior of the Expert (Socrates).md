When Thrasymachus categorizes injustice as a virtue and wisdom, how does Socrates use the behavioral concept of _outdoing ("getting the better of")_ to prove the opposite?
#### Notes
A wise craftsman only attempts to outdo the ignorant, never another wise practitioner (349e–350b). The just man shares this exact pattern, outdoing only the unjust (349c). The unjust man attempts to outdo everyone, aligning his behavior with the ignorant and bad (349d; 350b–c)
