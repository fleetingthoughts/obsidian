Why does the property qualification metric of Oligarchy represent a catastrophic systemic error in governance?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Competent rule in any craft (_techne_) requires specialized skill, not material wealth.<br>• _Argument_: Socrates uses the analogy of choosing ship pilots on the basis of property assessments. Because an oligarchy refuses to entrust rule to a poor man, even if he is a highly skilled pilot, it is argued that the city will make a disastrously poor sailing, proving that wealth qualification is a fundamental error in political navigation (Book 8, 551c–d).
