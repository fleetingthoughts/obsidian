Contrast the cognitive focus of the "lovers of sights and sounds" with that of the true philosopher.
#### Notes
Lovers of sights and sounds delight in beautiful colors, shapes, and the many changing particulars, but their thought is unable to perceive the nature of the "fair itself" (475d–476b). The true philosopher loves the whole of wisdom and is capable of seeing the singular Forms, recognizing the beautiful itself without confusing it with the things that participate in it (475b–c, 476b–c).
