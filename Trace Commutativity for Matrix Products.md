Let $A$ and $B$ be $n \times n$ matrices. Even though matrix multiplication is generally non-commutative ($AB \neq BA$), what special algebraic property or invariant do their products share when applying the trace operation, and how does transposition affect the trace?
#### Notes
The trace of the product is commutative, specifically $\text{tr}(AB) = \text{tr}(BA)$, and it is also invariant under transposition, $\text{tr}(A) = \text{tr}(A^t)$.
