Let $A$ and $B$ be $n \times n$ matrices. Although matrix multiplication is fundamentally non-commutative ($AB \neq BA$), the trace operation (the sum of the diagonal entries) imposes a strict algebraic invariant on their products.
#### Notes
The trace of the product is commutative, specifically $\text{tr}(AB) = \text{tr}(BA)$, and it is also invariant under transposition, $\text{tr}(A) = \text{tr}(A^t)$.
