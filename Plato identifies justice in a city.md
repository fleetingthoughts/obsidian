How does Socrates identify Civic Justice (_Dikaiosyne_) is the foundational virtue of the Kallipolis?
#### Notes
Socrates deduces
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: Justice is that which remains when wisdom, courage, and moderation have been discovered, acting as the power that enables them to come into being and preserves them once they have arisen (Book 4, 433b).<br>• _Argument_: Because the minding of one's own business (and not being a busybody) is the exact rule established at the city's founding to make the state good, it is argued that this practice of "one man, one job" is the ultimate definition of civic justice (Book 4, 432d-433b).
