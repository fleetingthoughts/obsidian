Prove: In an inner product space, if $\langle x, y \rangle = \langle x, z \rangle$ for all $x \in V$, then $y = z$.
#### Notes
$\langle x, y - z \rangle = 0$ for all $x$. Setting $x = y - z$ gives $\langle y - z, y - z \rangle = 0$, forcing $y - z = 0$, so $y = z$.
