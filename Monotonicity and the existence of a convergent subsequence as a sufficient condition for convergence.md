State the proposition regarding a sequence's monotonicity and its subsequence(s) that is sufficient for convergence.
#### Notes
Let $(a_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a monotonic sequence of real numbers. If there exists a subsequence $(a_{n_k})_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ that converges to a limit $L \in \mathbb{R}$, then the original sequence $(a_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ also converges to $L$. $$\lim_{k \to \infty} a_{n_k} = L \implies \lim_{n \to \infty} a_n = L$$
