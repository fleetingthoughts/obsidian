State the Generalized mean value theorem for functions $f$ and $g$.
#### Notes
If $f$ and $g$ are continuous on $[a, b]$ and differentiable on $(a, b)$, there exists a point $c \in (a, b)$ where $[f(b) - f(a)] g'(c) = [g(b) - g(a)] f'(c)$.
