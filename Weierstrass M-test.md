State the Weierstrass M-test for uniform convergence of a series of functions.
#### Notes
For each $n \in \mathbf{N}$, let $f_n$ be defined on $A \subseteq \mathbf{R}$, and let $M_n > 0$ satisfy $\vert{}f_n(x)\vert{} \le M_n$ for all $x \in A$. If $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} M_n$ converges, then $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} f_n$ converges uniformly on $A$.
