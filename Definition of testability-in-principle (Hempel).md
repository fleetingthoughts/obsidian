What is the principle of "testability-in-principle," and why is it a necessary condition for a scientific hypothesis?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** Testability-in-principle requires that a statement or theory T must logically yield test implications of the form "If C then E," even if the test conditions C cannot be technologically realized at the time the hypothesis is proposed. Without this, no conceivable empirical findings can ever agree or conflict with T, rendering it scientifically meaningless. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 3, p. 30)
