What is the supreme role of the Idea of the Good in both the visible and intelligible realms?
#### Notes
**Argued Position.**<br>• _Premise_: The Idea of the Good is the highest and final object of contemplation in the knowable realm, achieved only with great effort (Book 7, 517b-c).<br>• _Argument_: Once the Idea of the Good is seen, it must be logically concluded that it is the ultimate cause of all that is right and fair: in the visible realm, it gives birth to light and the sun, while in the intelligible realm, it is itself sovereign and directly provides truth and intelligence to the soul (Book 7, 517c).
