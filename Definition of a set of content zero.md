Define what it means for a subset $A \subseteq [a, b]$ to have content zero.
#### Notes
For every $\epsilon \gt 0$, there exists a finite collection of open intervals $\{O_1, O_2, \dots, O_N\}$ such that:

$$A \subseteq \bigcup_{n=1}^N O_n \quad \text{and} \quad \sum_{n=1}^N \vert{}O_n\vert{} \le \epsilon$$
