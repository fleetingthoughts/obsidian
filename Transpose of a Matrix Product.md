Let $A$ be an $m \times n$ matrix and $B$ be an $n \times p$ matrix. If the product $AB$ is transposed, the operation distributes over the matrices but reverses their sequential order
#### Notes
The transpose of the product is exactly the product of the transposes in the opposite order, formally stated as $(AB)^t = B^t A^t$.
