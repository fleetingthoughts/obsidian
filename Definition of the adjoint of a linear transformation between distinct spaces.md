Let $T: V \to W$ be a linear transformation between finite-dimensional inner product spaces $(V, \langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle_1)$ and $(W, \langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle_2)$. Define the adjoint $T^*: W \to V$.
#### Notes
The unique linear transformation $T^*: W \to V$ satisfying:

$$\langle T(x), y \rangle_2 = \langle x, T^*(y) \rangle_1 \quad \text{for all } x \in V, \, y \in W$$
