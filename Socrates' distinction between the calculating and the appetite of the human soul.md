Apply the principle of non-contradiction to the experience of a thirsty man who refuses to drink to prove the soul is divided.
#### Notes
Thirst itself naturally drives the soul solely toward drink (437d–438a). If something draws the soul back from drinking when it is thirsty, it must logically be a different part: the irrational "desiring" part is driven by affections, while the "calculating" part draws it back via reason (438d–439d).
