How does Socrates characterize the ontological status of the many particular things (e.g., fair things, doubles, big things)?
#### Notes
**Premise.** Socrates establishes that every particular thing (e.g., a beautiful object, a double unit, a heavy body) is fundamentally ambiguous because it will always simultaneously appear as its opposite (ugly, half, or light); like the children's riddle about the eunuch hitting the bat with a pumice stone, these particulars cannot be thought of fixedly as either being or not being, but rather "roll around" in between both (Book 5, 479a–d).
