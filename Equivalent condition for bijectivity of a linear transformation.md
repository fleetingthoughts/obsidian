State the equivalent conditions for bijectivity of a linear transformation $T:V \to W$.
#### Notes
If $V$ and $W$ are finite-dimensional and $\dim(V) = \dim(W)$, the following are equivalent: 
 1) $T$ is one-to-one. 
 2) $T$ is onto. 
 3) $\text{rank}(T) = \dim(V)$.
