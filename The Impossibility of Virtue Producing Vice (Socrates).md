When Polemarchus claims justice involves harming bad enemies, how does Socrates use the nature of _excellence (arete)_ to prove a just person cannot cause harm
#### Notes
Socrates establishes that harming an entity makes it worse with respect to its specific virtue (e.g., human virtue, which is justice) (335b–c). Because an expert cannot use their excellence to produce its opposite (a musician cannot use music to make someone unmusical), a just person cannot use justice to make someone unjust (335c–d). Therefore, a just man never harms anyone (335e).
