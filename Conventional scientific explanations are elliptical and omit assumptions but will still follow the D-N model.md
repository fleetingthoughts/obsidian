How does Hempel analyze the logical structure of "elliptical explanations" of the form "E because C," such as "the slush remained liquid because it was sprinkled with salt"?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Methodological Argument.** Elliptical explanations omit mention of certain assumptions that are taken for granted in context. The statement "the slush remained liquid because it had been sprinkled with salt" is structurally a D-N explanation that tacitly presupposes the universal law that the freezing point of water is lowered whenever salt is dissolved in it, as well as specific physical conditions (such as the temperature not dropping below a very low point). Once these tacit premises are made explicit, they form a complete D-N argument. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 5, p. 52)
