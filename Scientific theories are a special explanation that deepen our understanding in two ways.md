In what two ways does a good scientific theory deepen and broaden our understanding of empirical laws?
#### Notes
**Nature of Proposition: Conceptual Definition.** First, a theory deepens understanding by providing a systematically unified account of diverse phenomena, showing that seemingly unrelated empirical laws (like free fall, tides, and planetary motion) are manifestations of a common set of basic laws. Second, it deepens understanding by showing that previously established empirical laws do not hold strictly and unexceptionally, but rather hold only approximately and within a certain limited range of application. (_Philosophy of Natural Science_, Chapter 6, pp. 75-76)
