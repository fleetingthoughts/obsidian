Prove if $B$ be an $n \times m$ matrix with rank $m$. There exists an $m \times n$ matrix $A$ such that $AB = I_m$
#### Notes
$\text{rank}(B) = m$ implies $L_B: F^m \to F^n$ is injective. Thus, $L_B$ admits a left inverse transformation $U$. Letting $A$ be the matrix representation of $U$ yields $AB = I_m$.
