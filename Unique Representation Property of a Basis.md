Let $V$ be a vector space and $\beta = \{u_1, u_2, \dots, u_n\}$ be a set of distinct vectors in $V$. The set $\beta$ is a basis for $V$ if and only if every vector $v \in V$ can be uniquely expressed as a linear combination of the vectors in $\beta$ in the form $v = \sum_{i=1}^n a_i u_i$.
